'''Kendl Johnson''' is a main character in {{SA}}.

She is voiced by [[Yolanda Whittaker]].

==History==
===Background===
Kendl is [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] and [[Sean Johnson|Sweet's]] younger sister, and the older sister of the late Brian Johnson. Their mother is [[Beverly Johnson]] (who dies just before the game begins). She is also [[Cesar Vialpando]]'s girlfriend, and therefore affiliated with both Cesar's [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] and the [[Grove Street Families]].

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
{{Stub}}
After learning about [[Big Smoke]] and [[Ryder|Ryder's]] betrayal, CJ requests that Cesar bring Kendl to safety by fleeing from [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]]. Later in the storyline, Cesar pondered proposing to Kendl but the question of whether Kendl accepted or not remains unanswered.

Moving to the [[Doherty Garage|gas station/garage]] in [[Doherty (3D Universe)|Doherty]], [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]] alongside Carl, Cesar, and The Truth, she found the place could be reworked into something profitable, putting her despaired bother into an ease ([[Claude]] gave him that place as the reward for the [[Farewell, My Love...|race]] instead of a pink slip). The renovation into a successful chop shop went well after The Truth enlisted [[Jethro|a]] [[Dwaine|few]] [[Zero|acquaintances]] of his for the endeavor, though it was briefly interrupted by construction workers from the [[The Waste Ground|construction site]] next to the garage who harassed her, much to CJ's anger that [[Deconstruction|he confronted them for it.]]

==Character==
===Personality===
Kendl is depicted as intelligent and pragmatic which suggests that although her brothers turned to crime as a last resort to support themselves, Kendl could become successful within the bounds of the law which makes her the "brains" of CJ's business ventures. She possesses entrepreneurial talents, strong willpower, creativity, and a sense of leadership. In fact it was Kendl who came up with the idea of "Letting [[money]] work for you" and when CJ responded with, "I ain't going to no college man", she shot back that she will take care of it. She has little tolerance for whining or complaining or complacency which causes CJ and others to regard her as a bit bossy. This is exemplified in the mission [[Zeroing In]], when she discovers everyone sitting around playing poker and chastises them questioning if that was how they would build their business.

==Trivia==
*The only mission where Kendl appears outside of cutscenes is [[High Stakes, Low-Rider]]. During this mission, her [[Beta Content in GTA San Andreas|Beta]] model is used during regular gameplay. The inconsistency is fixed in {{TDE}}.
*Kendl's beta model wears a black #81 football jersey with "BROWN" on the back, blue track pants and white shoes. The model can still be found inside game files, and was edited slightly by [[Rockstar North]] into a [[Pedestrians|pedestrian]] model: "BROWN" was changed to "CROON", the "81" to "31", and black to purple.
*The only time Kendl calls [[Carl Johnson|Carl]] "CJ" in the storyline is during [[King in Exile]]. She can also call Carl CJ if the player loses the race in High Stakes, Low-Rider.
*Shortly before dying in the mission [[High Noon]], Officer [[Eddie Pulaski]] claims he wants to have sex with Kendl, leading Carl to say he was an "asshole to the end". In addition, during [[Yay Ka-Boom-Boom]], Cesar claims [[Ryder]] once tried to seduce Kendl.
