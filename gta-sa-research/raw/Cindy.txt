'''Cindy''' is a minor character in {{TBOGT}}.

==History==
===Background===
Cindy is a waitress at [[Maisonette 9]] working for [[Tony Prince]].

===Events of ''The Ballad of Gay Tony''===
Cindy is briefly seen during the mission [[Chinese Takeout]], and has a short amount of dialogue in [[Boulevard Baby]] after Tony orders her to give [[Luis Fernando Lopez]], [[Ray Bulgarin]], and [[Timur]] a round of drinks on the house.

When competing in the [[Champagne Drinking Contest]], Cindy brings the bottle of champagne up to Luis, and puts a lit sparkler into the neck of the bottle.

==Character==
===Appearance===
Cindy has the same character model as [[Cloe Parker]] and [[Shirley Harlingford]], though she has black hair whilst Parker and Harlingford are brunettes.
