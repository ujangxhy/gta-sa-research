'''Love Fist''' is a [[Scotland|Scottish]] {{WP|Heavy metal music|heavy metal}} band in the {{3D}} of the {{S}}. The band is prominently featured in {{VC}} and mentioned in many of the following games since.

==Description==
The band is comprised of [[Jezz Torrent (3D Universe)|Jezz Torrent]] ("Jizz" if said with a Scottish accent, a slang term for semen) and [[Willy]], [[Dick (3D Universe)|Dick]], and [[Percy (3D Universe)|Percy]] (all slang terms for the penis).

It is not revealed when the band were formed, but by 1984 [[V-Rock (3D Universe)|V-Rock]] was hosting a giveaway of Love Fist concert tickets and by 1986 they are on their "Steel Heart Stone Cold Prostate" tour, and being managed by [[Kent Paul]]. By 1992, they are only a three-man band, as seen on a [[Vinyl Countdown]] billboard as Willy has left the group (In reality, this is due to the fact that he doesn't have a character model, as the Love Fist members in the billboard are models from ''GTA Vice City''). The band broke up at least once in between 1992 and 1998, since there is a billboard visible in {{LCS}}, which advertises the band's comeback tour.

During in game dialogue in ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'', several secrets are revealed about the band members, lead singer Jezz Torrent apparently used to have sex with sheep, drummer Dick sleeps with bunny pajamas, guitarist Percy is a huge fan of Duran Duran and bassist Willy likes to wear woman's underwear. Jezz Torrent sings about cross-dressing on "Dangerous Bastard" and Tommy accuses the entire band of bisexuality, to which they do not offer a reply.

==Influence==
The name "Love Fist" is a reference to fisting, an extreme form of penetration.

The band may have been inspired by '70s British rock group {{WP|The Sweet}} because the music style and vocals are similar. Love Fist's appearance and lyrics seem to parody that of 1980s {{WP|Heavy metal music|heavy metal}} bands, specifically {{WP|New wave of British heavy metal|NWoBHM}} bands and American {{WP|Glam metal|hair metal}} bands, such as {{WP|Poison (American band)|Poison}}, {{WP|L.A. Guns}}, and {{WP|Warrant (American band)|Warrant}}.

The album ''The Number of the Breast'' is an obvious parody of the {{WP|Iron Maiden}} album ''{{WP|The Number of the Beast (album)|The Number of the Beast}}''.

Several of their songs make references to the culture surrounding heavy metal in the 1980's. Some songs, among which include ''"Dangerous Man - Dead Family,"'' ''"Satan's Pillows"'', and ''"Dangerous Bastard"'' appear to be in reference to the {{WP|Satanic panic}} prominent in the 1980's, where popular heavy metal bands like Twisted Sister, Mötley Crüe, and Quiet Riot were accused of teaching "immoral" lessons to youths. Other songs, like ''"A Broken Heart Can't Be Mended (Just Broken Again)"'' and ''"Fallen Stars on Shattered Dreams in the Rain"'' seem to reference the attempts by various 80's metal bands at power ballads. Another notable song is ''"Liver Buster"'', which seems to reference the prevalence of alcoholism in the 80's rock scene. As for the rest of their songs, they mainly appear to be sexual references (''"Chin Stanier"'', ''"Four Boys against Your Face"'', and ''"Beast Fist"'').

==Voice Actors==
Jezz Torrent was voiced by [[Kevin McKidd]], Percy by Paul Mackie, Dick by John Ritchie, and Willy by [[Mark Hanlon]]. Vice City's end credits also list [[Russell Foreman]] as Percy and [[Peter McKay]] as Dick.

==Events==
===''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City''===
While on tour during their stop in [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]], the band had security provided by [[Mitch Baker|Big Mitch Baker's]] [[Vice City Bikers|biker gang]].

[[Tommy Vercetti]] performs various tasks for the band, such as obtaining their "[[Love Juice (product)|Love Juice]]" (a mix of [[cocaine]], [[boomshine]], fizz bombs and petrol) and saving them from a [[The Psycho|murderous fan]] [[Psycho Killer|out to kill them]]. He also brings them [[Mercedes Cortez]] to "keep them company". Willy is the only band member that does not accompany the band when they [[Publicity Tour|go out]] into the city when Tommy chauffeurs them.

===''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
There are several references to Love Fist in {{SA}}, including a graffiti tag of their name in the [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]] [[Below the Belt Gym|gym]]. They are also seen on a billboard. [[Carl Johnson]] uses a Love Fist poster to hide the [[Caligula's Palace]] heist plans in [[The Four Dragons Casino]] which can be seen in the opening cutscene of [[Key to Her Heart]]. They are also mentioned on the radio station [[K-DST]] when the [[Tommy Smith (3D Universe)|host]] says, "''What ever happened to Love Fist?''" The biker pedestrian can also be heard asking the same question. A [[Dialogues in GTA San Andreas#White businessman|White businessman pedestrian]] may also be heard complaining about the cost of the new album saying, "''17 bucks for a new Love Fist CD?!''".

The songs "Dangerous Bastard" and "Fist Fury" are featured on the in-game radio station [[V-Rock (3D Universe)|V-Rock]]. A full version of "Dangerous Bastard" is also included on the official V-Rock soundtrack album, released as part of the ''[[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Official Soundtrack Box Set]]''. "Down Down Down" and "Fist Till Morning" are not fully featured in the game; a small portion of "Down Down Down" can be heard playing in the background during the opening [[cutscenes]] for the missions [[Love Juice]] and [[Publicity Tour]], while a small portion of "Fist Till Morning" can be heard during the Love Fist Tour commercial.

In early 2013, Rockstar Games remastered all four tracks and made them available for digital download as part of the ''[[Love Fist (album)|Love Fist EP]]'' via iTunes and Amazon.
