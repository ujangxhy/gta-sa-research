'''Running Dog''' is a mission in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]'' given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[Grove Street Families]] member [[Big Smoke|Melvin "Big Smoke" Harris]] from his [[Big Smoke's House|home]] in [[Idlewood]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Description==
Carl goes to Smoke's house. Upon arriving there, he finds [[Frank Tenpenny|Dr. Officer Frank Tenpenny]] and [[Eddie Pulaski|Officer Eddie Pulaski]] leaving. Smoke informs Carl that they are attempting to extract information from him. Smoke tells CJ that his cousin Mary is coming to the town from Mexico and he's going to pick her up and get challenged to a cousin by these gangster Vagos.

The two drive to a [[Marble Del Rancho|place]] to meet two [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]] [[Vagos gang members (Running Dog)|gang members]], with Carl realising what they're really there for. After a short mixed Spanish conversation, Smoke knocks one out using a [[Baseball Bat|baseball bat]] while the other runs away. Carl is tasked with chasing down and killing the Vago, who will run between buildings and over fences to try and lose Carl. Two Vagos members outside [[E-W Liquor]] will start shooting at Carl as he gets near. If the escaping Vago is not killed in time, he will enter a [[Sentinel]] as a passenger, and Carl has to get another vehicle and chase him down. The Sentinel starts off driving randomly, but if the mission is still not completed after three minutes, the Vagos will start driving towards [[Las Colinas]], where a short cutscene will play showing the Vago exiting the passenger side of the car and running away on foot, failing the mission.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Get into Big Smoke's <font color=#484576>'''car.'''</font>
*Drive Big Smoke to the <font color=#D1BC86>'''meet.'''</font> If you need a weapon, go to Emmet's house.
*Chase down the <font color=#AB3736>'''gang member'''</font>.

==Oversights==
{{Main|Grove Street Games/Errors}}
In the 2014 release of the Mobile version, the outsourced [[Grove Street Games]] studio (then known as War Drum Studios) purposely disabled the "Super Sprint" feature introduced by this mission for the touchscreen controls.

In the 2021 release of ''[[Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy - The Definitive Edition|The Definitive Edition]]'' version by Grove Street Games (which is essentially a remastered version of the Mobile version for consoles and PC with Touchscreen UI hidden by default), the "Super Sprint" remains absent from the game. The instruction prompts during the chase will still counterintuitively mention "Super Sprint" despite the player not being able to perform it in this version.

==Trivia==
===Gameplay===
*If the Sentinel is destroyed or the driver is killed before the Vago runs to it, he will just flee from the scene with a random NPC scream, giving the player time to kill him.
*If the running Vago is killed while in sight of the Sentinel, after the mission is completed, the Sentinel driver will drive the vehicle away rapidly.
*The Sentinel is locked if the driver is waiting for the Vago in it. If the driver is killed by whatever reason, Carl can enter the vehicle and it can be kept after killing the Vago, but it will lock itself if the player simply gets out of the car and fails to leave the door open.
*After failing the mission, if Carl has over 50% fat, he will receive a phone call from Smoke shortly afterwards, who tells Carl that he is "letting himself go" and needs to visit the Gym, and Carl declines his suggestion. This can be seen [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l6ObsxniZOc here].
*This is the only time the interior of Big Smoke's house is seen (at least part of it). Outside of this cutscene, if managing to go through the building, the interior will be absent, as it is scripted to spawn only in the cutscene.
*Even though there's no monetary reward for completing the mission, the Vagos member drops a large amount of [[Money|cash]] upon death and a [[Pistol (3D Universe)|pistol]], similarly to [[Crack Dealers|drug dealer]] [[pedestrians]].
*On some occasions, if the player attempts to shoot the running Vago in the head after he gets into the vehicle, not only will he drop money and a pistol, but also an extra [[Micro SMG (3D Universe)|Micro SMG]] that he has used to perform a drive-by shooting on CJ.
*The destination of the Vagos ride is the house with the green door in Las Colinas. This can be seen [https://youtu.be/IZv0Mth9NQQ?t=233 here]. Allowing the Vagos to reach the house will result in a mission failure.

===Story===
*The name of the mission is likely a reference to the pejorative {{WP|Running dog}}, which refers to an unprincipled person who helps someone more powerful than themselves. This is likely a foreshadowing of Smoke's betrayal, describing his relationship with Tenpenny which was visually hinted at in the opening cutscene of the mission.
*Big Smoke says his cousin is called "Mary", which is a reference to Mary Jane, slang for marijuana.
*The line the second Vagos member uses roughly translates to, "fuck you, dumbass," and afterwards, "fuck your mother, asshole."
*In this mission, it is revealed that Big Smoke knows how to speak (some) Spanish.
*Earlier during the game's development, this mission also required Carl to drive Smoke back home. CJ asked Smoke about Tenpenny's visit at the start of the mission, which Smoke replied that he does not know who Tenpenny is. It is speculated that part was cut because it was strongly foreshadowing Smoke's [[The Green Sabre|betrayal]]. The part can be seen [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1hnZysidtkI here].
*The song in the background during the cutscene of Smoke meeting with the Vagos is "Sidewalk Killer" by I-Roy, which can be heard on [[K-JAH West]].
