'''Flint Range''' is a small farm in {{SA}}, located in [[Flint County (3D Universe)|Flint County]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Description==
Flint Range encompasses exclusively of farmland, including three fields to the northwest, northeast and southeast, and a farmhouse in the center. The farmland is presumably owned by [[Helena Wankstein]], who can be [[Girlfriends in GTA San Andreas|dated]] over the course of the game, as the player can meet her or "have some coffee" at the farmhouse.

Located nearby is an unnamed [[Filling Stations|gas station]], an [[RS Haul]] [[RS Haul Depot|depot]], and the [[Flint Intersection]].
