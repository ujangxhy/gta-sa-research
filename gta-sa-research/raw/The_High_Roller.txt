'''The High Roller''' is a [[casino]] that appears in {{SA}}.

==Description==
The casino is located along [[The Strip]] in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]]. The High Roller is situated between [[Pirates in Men's Pants]] and [[The Pink Swan]]. While inaccessible to [[Carl Johnson|CJ]], the casino is unique for having an escalator and a {{WP|moving walkway}}.

==Influence==
The casino is based on {{WP|Horseshoe Las Vegas|Bally's}} in {{WP|Las Vegas, Nevada}}, which was rebranded to the Horseshoe Las Vegas in 2022.
