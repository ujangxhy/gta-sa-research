'''Ran Fa Li''' (Traditional Chinese: '''李然發''', Simplified Chinese: '''李然发''', Pinyin: '''''Lǐ Rán Fā'''''), also known as '''Farlie''', is a supporting character in {{SA}}.

He is voiced by [[Hunter Platin]].

==History==
===Background===
Ran Fa Li is the leader of the Red Gecko Tong [[San Fierro Triads|Triads]] in [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]]. [[Wu Zi Mu]] goes to great lengths to impress Ran Fa Li, presumably due to Ran Fa Li's higher ranking in the Triads.

He has no speaking lines throughout the game, instead communicating through some grunts that have to be interpreted by [[Su Xi Mu|his assistant and translator]].

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
[[Carl Johnson]] does a few jobs for Wu Zi Mu on behalf of Ran Fa Li, namely [[Ran Fa Li (mission)|retrieving an important package]] from the [[Easter Bay Airport|airport]] and helping Ran Fa Li leave [[Chinatown (GTA San Andreas)|Chinatown]] safely by driving [[Rancher (3D Universe)|a vehicle]] [[Lure (GTA San Andreas)|out to the countryside as a decoy]] to lure the [[Da Nang Boys]] coming to kill him away from Wu Zi Mu's [[Wu Zi Mu's Apartment|apartment]], in turn earning him the trust of Ran Fa Li.

Ran Fa Li is also the third stakeholder in the [[Four Dragons Casino]], which is located at the southern tip of [[The Strip]] in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]] (along with Wu Zi Mu and Carl Johnson).
