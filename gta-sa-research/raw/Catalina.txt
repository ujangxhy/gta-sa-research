'''Catalina''' is the main [[antagonist]] of {{III}} and a main character in {{SA}}.

She is voiced by [[Cynthia Farrell]].

==History==
===Background===
Catalina was a criminal in [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]] in ''GTA San Andreas'' and the leader of the [[Colombian Cartel]] in ''GTA III''. She is the cousin of [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] leader [[Cesar Vialpando]]. Catalina is of mixed [[Dominican Republic|Dominican]] and [[Colombia]]n descent{{Ref|name=Nationality}}. 

According to Catalina, she had a troubled childhood, as she was raised by her abusive step father who gave "nothing to his step daughter but stale bread". This would result into her developing hatred for men, claiming that all men are the same, having no respect for women.

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Catalina began her criminal activities some time prior to [[1992]], when she [[First Date (GTA San Andreas)|met]] [[Carl Johnson]] at the recommendation of her cousin, [[Cesar Vialpando]], meeting at [[The Welcome Pump]] in [[Dillimore]], with Catalina fending off two men with a [[Knife|knife]]. The two become romantically involved (although, in CJ's case, it was reluctant), with Catalina putting Carl through an extreme form of BDSM before committing a robbery. The two begin to rob various "soft targets" identified by Catalina, including a [[Gasso]] [[Tanker Commander|gas station]] in [[Dillimore]], a [[Small Town Bank|bank]] in [[Palomino Creek]], an [[Inside Track]] [[Against All Odds|betting shop]] in [[Montgomery (3D Universe)|Montgomery]] and a [[Local Liquor Store|small liquor store]] in [[Blueberry]]. Their relationship, however, becomes strained over Carl's desire for money to try to free his brother, [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet]], who was [[The Green Sabre|incarcerated]] by the [[Los Santos Police Department (3D Universe)|LSPD]]. After the two separate, she begins a relationship with [[Claude]] and "helps" him in two races involving Carl and [[Wu Zi Mu]]. Johnson, who wins both races, wins a [[Doherty Garage|garage]] in [[Doherty (3D Universe)|Doherty]], [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] from Claude, who leaves with Catalina on a nine-year journey to [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]]; Catalina, however, continues to phone Carl and tease him about her relationship with Claude. The last phone call is received immediately after [[End of the Line]], in which Catalina can be heard having sex with Claude to tease Carl.

===After the Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Catalina and Claude, after leaving San Andreas, begin a nine-year journey to Liberty City, going through many [[United States|American]] states committing bank robberies, including Texas and New Mexico.{{Ref|name=Nationality}} The two continue on their journey to Liberty City, reaching their destination by October [[2001]].{{Ref|name=Nationality}}

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto III''===
After arriving in Liberty City, Catalina begins discussions with and becomes the leader of the [[Colombian Cartel]], with some of their members (including [[Miguel]]), rob a [[Liberty City Bank|bank]] in the [[Ghost Town|city]]. After robbing the bank, she kills an [[Unnamed GTA III robbery accomplice|unnamed accomplice]] and shoots Claude, leaving him for dead, and explaining that he is "small time", [[Introduction (GTA III)|ending]] a nine-year relationship. She continues her criminal activities with the Cartel, increasing the production of [[SPANK]] and attacking the [[Yakuza (3D Universe)|Yakuza]] on [[Staunton Island]] and the [[Leone Crime Family]] on [[Portland]]. During this period, Claude managed to [[Give Me Liberty|escape]] from an [[Liberty City Police Department (3D Universe)|LCPD]] convoy with, unintentionally, help from the Colombian Cartel (who were seeking to capture the [[Old Oriental Gentleman]]) and began working for Leone made man and nightclub/brothel owner [[Luigi Goterelli]]. After proving himself, Claude begins to work for the Family [[Gang Leaders|Don]], [[Salvatore Leone]], who has him [[Cutting the Grass|follow]] [[Luigi's Sex Club 7]] barman [[Curly Bob]] to [[Portland Harbor]], where he is seen to sell Leone secrets to Catalina and Miguel. Salvatore then has Claude and [[8-Ball]] [[Bomb Da Base: Act II|destroy]] a freighter used by the Cartel to produce SPANK.

Claude later flees to Staunton Island and begins to work for Yakuza co-leader [[Asuka Kasen]] who begins to attack the Cartel following the [[Waka-Gashira Wipeout!|death]] of her brother [[Kenji Kasen|Kenji]] at the hands of a "Cartel assassin," which was really Claude working on orders from [[Donald Love]]. Love later sends Claude to [[Grand Theft Aero|collect a package]] from a construction site in [[Fort Staunton]], where he once again meets Catalina and Miguel, with Catalina shooting Miguel in the back and escaping empty-handed. Asuka, desperate to exact revenge for her brother's death, begins to torture Miguel for information, with Claude then using the information to attack Cartel interests, including [[Espresso-2-Go!|destroying stands]] selling SPANK. During his time on Staunton Island, Claude also works for the [[Uptown Yardies]], but he is later betrayed by their leader, [[King Courtney]], who [[Kingdom Come|sends him into a trap]] organized by Catalina. Claude manages to escape.

Catalina later [[Ransom (GTA III)|returns to the construction site and kills both Asuka and Miguel, and kidnaps]] [[Maria Latore]], using her as a hostage and demanding a [[Money|$]]500,000 ransom be brought to the [[Cartel Mansion|Cartel headquarters]] in [[Cedar Grove]]. Claude decides to [[The Exchange (GTA III)|take the ransom money]] to Catalina and manages to escape an ambush, set up by Catalina, who escapes in a [[Helicopter (GTA III)|helicopter]] and keeps Maria hostage. Claude follows Catalina to [[Cochrane Dam]], where he destroys the helicopter with a [[Rocket Launcher (3D Universe)|Rocket Launcher]], killing Catalina and severely weakening the Cartel.

==Character==
===Personality===
{{Quote|Sorry, babe. I'm an ambitious girl and you, you're just small time.|Catalina as she betrayed [[Claude]] in the [[Introduction (GTA III)|Introduction]].}}
Catalina is described in various terms. In short, she is a psychotic, self-centered, hotheaded, destructive, and reckless woman. The very dark, disturbing undertones of her personality suggest that she is mentally unstable. She has an extremely short temper and can very quickly become irate in a matter of seconds. She frequently insults and verbally abuses anyone she comes across, including those close to her (as seen with Carl and Claude), and has no regard for virtually anyone other than herself. 

It is strongly implied in passing that she is a sadist and a cannibal; some of Catalina's psyche is brought to light during her robberies with Carl, and she often shoots at [[pedestrians]], police, and anyone who gets in her way.

{{Quote|All you little men are scared of strong women! If we're passionate, you say we're crazy. If you're upset, you say we're hysterical. We sleep with men, we're sluts. If we don't put out, we're frigid bitches.|Catalina to [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] after her rampage.}}

By Catalina's own admission, she is a {{WP|Misandry|misandrist}}. She considers men weaker than her, and this hatred most likely stems from her stepfather's cruel treatment of her as a child. As such, Catalina views herself as a "strong" woman and sees men as nothing more than a stepping stone to further her ambitions. This is evident by her treatment of the two protagonists [[Carl Johnson]] and [[Claude]], whom she engaged in a romantic relationship with for, in her own words, "business" reasons. She often commands her partners to commit various crimes and robberies together with her, and would often ask if they were not "man" enough to the task when they questioned the collateral damage she caused.

In addition, Catalina also has a hypocritical approach towards her (former) romantical partner-in-crime. She is very assertive towards her then-partner Carl and would often coerce him into BDSM activities against his will. While later she would insult Carl for treating her like a "whore", due to her believing that Carl only cares about money and sex, she is not much better herself, as she is the one who ordered Carl to perform various robberies and take as much money as possible as a way to express his "love" towards her.

After Catalina ends her relationship with Carl, she is involved in a running gag in the later half of the story where she would phone Carl in an attempt to make him "jealous", as she believes Carl is still having some romantic feelings towards her. This tendency would extend to her second partner Claude as well, as during her final encounter with him, she asks if he came to rescue [[Maria Latore]] who she's held in ransom, or to take her back with him.

{{Quote|You have been a busy boy! But you haven't learned, I'm not to be trusted.|Catalina [[The Exchange (GTA III)|moments before she is killed]] by [[Claude]].}}

Outside of her issue with men, Catalina is a cunning and ambitious criminal, having made connections with the [[Colombian Cartel]] and its leader [[Miguel]] before betraying Claude at the beginning of ''GTA III''. 

Describing herself as an "ambitious girl", Catalina has no qualms in betraying her accomplices or even her own boyfriend Claude if it means it will further or protect her own interests. This is most apparent by her treatment of Miguel, who she shot and left for dead as he was forced to return [[Donald Love]]'s package after being threatened by Claude, and would later on brutally murder him by impaling him with a metal rod.

At the end of the ''GTA III'', Catalina once again betrays Claude after she took $500,000 ransom for Maria and ordered her men to kill him, mockingly stating that he hasn't learned that she's "not to be trusted". This however proved to be a mistake, as the well-prepared Claude managed to overpower Catalina's men and kill her afterwards.

===Appearance===
Catalina has different physical representations in the two games she appears in. In ''GTA III'', she has light brown skin and hazel eyes, while in ''GTA San Andreas'', she has medium brown skin and brown eyes.

Catalina's top and appearance is noticeably different from the original ''GTA III'' [[PlayStation 2|PS2]] release in later ''GTA III'' releases. In the [[Personal Computer|PC]] version, she has a lock of hair on her forehead and a ponytail. In the [[Xbox (console)|Xbox]] and 10th Anniversary version, she also wears a white tube top and purple necklace with the black leather jacket, green pants with her pink underwear sticking out behind her, black platform shoes, has a lower back [[Tattoos|tattoo]] and a navel piercing. Her model is also notably higher polygon, full mesh (not split) and uses a much bigger texture, unlike PS2.

In ''The Definitive Edition'', her hair, face, and clothes are based on the PC version in the introduction cutscene, while in the rest of the game, she is based on the classic PS2 model.

===Influence===
Catalina appears to be inspired by the late real-life American criminal {{WP|Bonnie and Clyde|Bonnie Elizabeth Parker}} as evidenced by the Red County robberies chapter in {{SA}} and her [[Liberty Tree Editorial/Have You Seen This Woman?|backstory given]] in the promotional website for {{III}}. Bonnie and her partner Clyde Chestnut Barrow would later serve as the inspiration for [[Lucia Caminos]] and [[Jason Duval]], the protagonistic duo of {{VI}}.

==Trivia==
===General===
*Catalina is one of two [[antagonist]]s to be related to the [[tritagonist]], the other being [[Chan Jaoming]].
*Catalina, in chronological order, was the last antagonist to be killed in the {{3D}}.
*She is similar to later antagonist of {{IV}}, [[Dimitri Rascalov]], in that they will betray even their closest associates in order to further their own power.
*In the first [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] of [[Farewell, My Love...]], Catalina revealed she meant she loves Carl when she said "just business". She also said dating Claude was only business in [[The Exchange (GTA III)|The Exchange]].

===''Grand Theft Auto: III''===
*She is one of only four antagonists in the {{S}} to be introduced before the [[protagonist]], as in the [[Introduction (GTA III)|Introduction]], she is shown fleeing from the bank with an unknown accomplice before [[Claude]] appears and destroys a security camera with a [[Shotgun (3D Universe)|Shotgun]]. The others are [[Miguel]] from the same [[Grand Theft Auto III|game]], [[Sonny Forelli]] from {{VC}}, and [[Frank Tenpenny]] from [[The Introduction|Grand Theft Auto San Andreas: The Introduction]].
*In the opening scene of {{III}} when Catalina appears, she does not have an accent while speaking to Claude, although for the rest of the game (as well as in {{SA}}), she speaks with an accent.

===''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
*Catalina uses male [[Pedestrians|pedestrian]] animations in most of the robbery missions, except in [[Small Town Bank]] and the interim missions where the player has to choose the robbery mission, where she uses female pedestrian animations.
