'''Photo Opportunity''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] leader [[Cesar Vialpando]] from the [[Doherty Garage|gas station/garage]] in [[Doherty (3D Universe)|Doherty]], [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

This mission is the introduction of a [[San Fierro Triads]] mission strand, although they are not introduced until the subsequent mission.

==Mission==
Carl receives a phone call from Cesar Vialpando. Cesar had been tracking a [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] car, most likely travelling to [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] to acquire cocaine. Cesar wants Carl to come with him, so he makes his way to Cesar's location near [[Blueberry]] in [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]]. The pair get in Cesar's [[Savanna]] and travel towards [[Angel Pine]], where the car was last seen.

After entering Angel Pine, Carl and Cesar position themselves on a rooftop overlooking a [[Cluckin' Bell (3D Universe)|Cluckin' Bell]] restaurant. Carl whips out a [[camera]] and plans to take photographs, believing this to be a secret meeting. Soon afterwards, a brown [[Picador]] pulls up. Carl's former ally [[Ryder]], along with a Ballas member step out of the vehicle. Another man arrives in a [[ZR-350]]. Cesar identifies him as [[T-Bone Mendez]] from the [[San Fierro Rifa]]. Shortly followed thereafter, is [[Mike Toreno|a mysterious man in a suit]], arriving in a [[Washington]]. Then finally, [[Jizzy B.|a fourth man]] arrives in a [[Broadway]], joining the others. He is identified as a pimp by Carl, but neither Cesar or Carl recognise him. After taking photos of all four men, Cesar and Carl leave Angel Pine, stopping by a filling station. Cesar drops Carl off, saying he'll meet him back at San Fierro.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Go and pick up '''<span style="color:#484576">Cesar</span>'''.
*Get in the '''<span style="color:#484576">car</span>'''.
*Head out to '''<span style="color:#D1BC86">Angel Pine</span>'''.
*Get up on the '''<span style="color:#D1BC86">roof</span>'''.
*Photograph the face of each target, but be quick about it and be sure to zoom in!

==Reward==
The reward for this mission is an increase in [[respect]]. The mission [[Jizzy (mission)|Jizzy]] is unlocked. After the mission, the game will inform the player of the availability of [[Snapshots]] located around San Fierro.

==Trivia==
*During [[Beta Content in GTA San Andreas|development of the game]], this mission had a completely different walkthrough. The player was originally tasked with stealing a helicopter to meet Cesar, and the photos would also have been taken whilst airborne. While watching over the meeting of the Loco Syndicate, Carl and Cesar shout to each other, presumably to make themselves heard over the sound of the helicopter's rotors, before the mission was changed without the dialogue being re-recorded. This is also evident when CJ states the mission "was some heavy shit", contrasting to the somewhat mundane car drive.
**Unused text strings in the <code>american.gxt</code> file reveal that the Ballas noticed Cesar tailing them and shot up his car, explaining why he had to stop and is seen checking the vehicle. The dialogue is left unused so the player can continue the trip by riding Cesar's (intact) red Savanna all the way to Angel Pine, instead of using the aforementioned helicopter to pick him up.
**A few lines were also cut, suggesting that CJ would have discovered Ryder's betrayal here and not in [[The Green Sabre]], which can be seen {{URL|1=https://youtu.be/QRxpLx0Oo-M?si=Q6Z-znvNAXP507UR&t{{=}}525|2=here}}.
*The [[Loco Syndicate]]'s vehicles – Ryder's [[Picador]], Jizzy's [[Broadway]], Mike Toreno's [[Washington]], and T-Bone Mendez's [[ZR-350]] – are all-proof and can be obtained.
*CJ cannot look backwards while on the rooftop with the camera active.
*Sometimes, after the mission is passed, the player's camera may be able to take an infinite number of photos. After the camera is lost and reobtained, this no longer occurs.
