'''Ganton''' is a neighborhood of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Description==
The neighborhood is bordered by [[Idlewood]] to the west, [[East Los Santos (3D Universe)|East Los Santos]] to the north, [[Willowfield]] to the south and [[East Beach]] to the east.

Ganton is the main stronghold of the [[Grove Street Families]], as [[Grove Street (3D Universe)|Grove Street]] is located here, and is populated by many Grove Street Family gang members. These include [[Carl Johnson]], [[Sweet Johnson]] and [[Ryder|Lance "Ryder" Wilson]]. Former residents revealed at the beginning of ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]'' include [[Beverly Johnson|Beverly]] and [[Brian Johnson]] (both deceased) and [[Big Smoke|Melvin "Big Smoke" Harris]] (who moved to Idlewood in 1989). The area is majority African-American.

Ganton has a very high crime rate, with prostitution, gang violence, car crime (especially [[carjacking]]s), poverty and [[drugs]] (especially [[cocaine|crack-cocaine]]) as the main issues. Upon nightfall many [[prostitutes]], Grove Street gangbangers, and [[Crack Dealers|drug dealers]] will spawn.

Because of this, [[Police Maverick (3D Universe)|Police Mavericks]] (also known as "ghetto birds") regularly patrol the area, flashing searchlights on gang members and other criminals. These helicopters will not pursue Carl unless he has a 3-star wanted level or higher. Vehicles may occasionally travel onto Grove Street, either performing U-turns or parking in random driveways.

On [[Ganton Bridge|the bridge]] going over Grove Street, if the player is driving fast enough, they can crash through a particular section of the wall and land onto the cul-de-sac below (this happened to [[Frank Tenpenny]] in the final mission of ''GTA San Andreas'', "[[End of the Line]]").

Ganton is structured as two different areas. The first area is highly residential, consisting of many small, single-family houses, a bar ([[Ten Green Bottles]]), and a long street from the Ganton/Idlewood border which runs and ends into Grove Street. There are two turns here, a small bridge which takes the player into East Los Santos and a small road that takes the player into the second half of Ganton.

The first area primarily consists of pedestrians (especially Grove Street itself). The second area is much busier, containing more traffic, and a long busy road which stretches from [[East Beach]] to Idlewood. The second area also contains housing projects known as the [[Southwest Ganton Projects]], a southern road which takes the player into [[Willowfield]], and a long, raised bridge which takes the player over Grove Street and into East Los Santos.

There is a higher level of gang violence at the Ganton/Idlewood border. The two neighbourhoods are separated by the [[Brown Streak Railroad]]. This is because Idlewood is [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] territory while Ganton is Grove Street Families territory. It is not uncommon to see Grove Street/Ballas shootouts from across the train tracks.

==History==
===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Ganton is the main stronghold of the Grove Street Families, but begins to come under increasing pressure from Ballas gang members (especially through the crack cocaine epidemic), hoping to take the area for themselves. This is enforced by Big Smoke, Ryder and [[B Dup|Mark "B-Dup" Wayne]], who are secretly affiliated with the Ballas through a clandestine deal. Drugs have become a large problem in Ganton, mainly through Smoke and Ryder buying drugs from the [[Loco Syndicate]] ([[Mike Toreno]], [[T-Bone Mendez]] and [[Jizzy B.]]).

Ganton eventually falls into the hands of the Ballas, after Carl Johnson is taken to [[Angel Pine]] by [[C.R.A.S.H.]] and Sweet Johnson is taken to a prison hospital during the mission [[The Green Sabre]]. The Grove Street Families disband with no leadership, although former gang members continue to wear the green colors. They more than likely continue to reside in Ganton, although they are not visible on the streets during Carl and Sweet's absence, instead, [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] will start to form a presence on the streets. The former Grove Street gang members, under the influence of Big Smoke, cooperate with the Ballas and [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]].

Carl and Sweet ([[Home Coming|released]] via Mike Toreno) later return to Ganton, which is in disarray due to the influx of drugs and Ballas. They reclaim Ganton for the Grove Street Families during the mission [[Home Coming]] (re-establishing the Grove Street Families as a major force in the Los Santos gang underworld, and use Ganton as a base for attacking other districts (such as [[Idlewood]]). Ganton, along with the rest of Los Santos, is later plagued by [[Los Santos Riots|riots]] following the acquittal of [[Frank Tenpenny]], although Carl and Sweet manage to "lock down" Grove Street, and bring stability to Ganton as a whole. Ganton also serves as the location of Frank Tenpenny's death, when he dies from injuries sustained when the Fire Truck he is driving lands in Grove Street after crashing off the bridge going over the street.

==Influence==
The name is a play on real life city {{WP|Compton, California}}, located within Los Angeles County. However, the housing projects areas where B-Dup formerly resided is more based on and partly resembles the {{WP|Imperial Courts}} Housing Projects in {{WP|Watts, Los Angeles|Watts}}, Los Angeles. Likewise, the main road in Ganton primarily resembles {{WP|Rosecrans Avenue}} in Compton. The name "Ganton" may come from a district in {{WP|Edinburgh}} (the Scottish city where the [[Rockstar North]] HQ is located) named "{{WP|Granton, Edinburgh|Granton}}".

==Gang Control==
Ganton is a stronghold for the Grove Street Families and it remains one of their few territories in eastern Los Santos after the gang's decline. Ganton is often attacked by the Ballas and the Vagos as the neighbourhood is completely surrounded by their territories.

In the mission [[Drive-Thru]], Carl, Ryder, Smoke and Sweet were dining at a [[Cluckin' Bell (3D Universe)|Cluckin' Bell]] in [[Willowfield]], and spotted a Ballas' [[Voodoo (3D Universe)|Voodoo]] heading to Grove Street, the four successfully gave chase to the invading Ballas and destroyed their car, killing the intruders.

Another attempt, depicted in the mission "[[House Party]]", three huge swarms of Ballas tried to attack Grove Street during a house party hosted by Jeffrey Cross, AKA OG Loc. Going through all possible alleyways and bridges to take the Families by surprise. Later on, the GSF [[The Green Sabre|lost a fight against the Ballas resulting in Sweet's arrest]] and resulted in the GSF being completely disbanded in [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], and [[Ganton]] falling into the Ballas control.

Near the end of the game, Sweet is released from prison and orders Carl to take him back to Grove Street, there, the two brothers in a cooperative effort [[Home Coming|eliminate all the Ballas in the Grove Street within Ganton, taking the neighborhood back as a GSF territory again]].
