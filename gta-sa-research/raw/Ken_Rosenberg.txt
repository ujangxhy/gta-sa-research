'''Ken Rosenberg''' is the [[deuteragonist]] in {{VC}} and a main character in {{SA}}. He is also indirectly mentioned in {{VCS}}.

He is voiced by [[William Fichtner]].

==History==
===Background===
Ken Rosenberg successfully graduated as a lawyer, although he is suspected of cheating on his exams, and established his own business, [[K. Rosenberg & Co.]], setting up the firm in [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]], with the company's office being inside the [[Hotel Harrison]] in [[Washington Beach (3D Universe)|Washington Beach]]. He started working for the [[Forelli Crime Family]] at some point in [[1978]], aged 25.{{Ref|name=birth}} At one point, he tried to intimidate a child and couldn't do it.

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories''===
By [[1984]], his company has begun to represent the [[VNN|Vice News Network]] legally, with the company advising people, through a VNN news report, to quote the second amendment of the [[United States|American]] constitution if they are prosecuted for illegally using a firearm, after open gun fights in the [[Downtown Vice City (3D Universe)|Downtown]] district (seen during the mission [[Nice Package]]). 

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City''===
By [[1986]], he has also established ties with the [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]] based Forelli Crime Family, an Italian-American mob, and has begun to represent them legally. He helps [[Sonny Forelli]], the suspected [[Gang Leaders|Don]] of the family, in establishing his family in Vice City by [[In The Beginning...|setting up a drug deal]] between the [[Vance Crime Family]]'s [[Victor Vance|Victor]] and [[Lance Vance]] and Forelli Family representatives [[Tommy Vercetti]], [[Harry]] and [[Lee]]. The deal is ambushed, but Rosenberg and Vercetti escape, with Rosenberg hiding in his office, even missing [[Juan Cortez|Colonel Juan Cortez']] [[The Party|yacht party]], instead sending Vercetti to meet the man who set up the deal and later to [[Back Alley Brawl|find out]] who ambushed the deal. Rosenberg had, before the ambushed drug deal, been representing [[Giorgio Forelli]], who was facing charges of fraud, and sends Vercetti to [[Jury Fury|intimidate]] two jurors. He had also begun representing real estate mogul [[Avery Carrington]], who Vercetti later begins to work for after causing a [[Riot (GTA Vice City)|riot]] at the [[Spand Express]] depot.

Vercetti eventually stops working for Rosenberg, who begins to represent him and bail him from the [[Vice City Police Department (3D Universe)|Vice City Police Department]] police stations across the city, with Rosenberg later attempting to give Vercetti advice about earning more money and about a planned [[The Job|bank heist]]. He later sides with Vercetti when Sonny Forelli arrives in Vice City and, along with Lance Vance, a former ally of Vercetti, [[Keep Your Friends Close...|attempts to take over Vercetti's business empire]]. The two become close friends, although Rosenberg's [[cocaine]] addiction later drives a wedge between the two.

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Tommy Vercetti and Ken Rosenberg continue to drift apart in the early 1990s, with Rosenberg eventually entering rehabilitation at the [[Fort Carson Medical Center]] in [[Fort Carson (3D Universe)|Fort Carson]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]]. His rehabilitation is successful, but his friendship with Vercetti is irreparably damaged, and he is also disbarred as a lawyer. Rosenberg, however, immediately falls into the clutches to Liberty City's three mafia families, the Forelli Crime Family, [[Leone Crime Family]] and the [[Sindacco Crime Family]], who have him run [[Caligula's Casino]], a mafia run casino in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], with all three families having a stake in the casino.

Rosenberg is informed by [[Salvatore Leone]], Don of the Leone Family, that he wants his money back quickly, whilst he expectantly waits for one family to kill him and blame one of the other families. Rosenberg begins to hide in his office once again, with only [[Tony]], a talking parrot, for company. [[Kent Paul]] and his client [[Maccer]] later [[Don Peyote|find]] Rosenberg and introduce him to [[Carl Johnson]], who begins to help Rosenberg out of his situation, including [[Intensive Care|saving]] [[Johnny Sindacco]] from a Forelli attack, though Johnny later [[The Meat Business|dies]] of a heart attack after he remembers his [[Fender Ketchup|previous encounter]] with Carl.

Carl later works for both Rosenberg and Salvatore Leone to kill more Sindacco and Forelli Family mobsters, both in [[Freefall|San Andreas]] and [[Saint Mark's Bistro|Liberty City]]. Eventually Carl helps Rosenberg, as well as the by now trapped Kent Paul and Maccer, to escape from Salvatore Leone, telling them to lie low. Rosenberg, who began to use cocaine, also seemingly has flashbacks, once stating "Just like old times, hey Tommy?", in reference to Tommy Vercetti, leading Carl to question, "Who the fuck is Tommy?"

==Character==
===Influence===
Rosenberg is undoubtedly heavily influenced by {{WP|Sean Penn}}'s character Dave Kleinfeld from the [[1993]] movie ''{{WP|Carlito's Way}}''. Both worked as lawyers, helped the protagonists, were heavily addicted to cocaine and both did something that made the protagonist sever their ties with them (in Kleinfeld's case, he was going to testify against Carlito and Tommy severed his ties with Rosenberg because of his drug addiction). The interior of Rosenberg's office in Vice City is also remarkably similar to Dave Klenfield's office.

==Trivia==
*During the mission [[The Meat Business]] in ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'', he will sometimes yell out "This is so exciting, Tommy! It's just like old times!", apparently still missing his days working alongside [[Tommy Vercetti]] in [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]].
*According to artwork, Ken Rosenberg originally wore the same soiree suit as he does in the game, but wore a yellow shirt underneath.
*In ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'', Ken is not seen outside of full-motion [[cutscenes]], except for the mission [[In The Beginning...]]
*Ken's name is misspelled as "Rosenburg" on the official {{URL|http://www.stephenbliss.com/|website}} of [[Rockstar Games]]' artist [[Stephen Bliss]]. Whether this is his beta name or just a typo is unknown.
