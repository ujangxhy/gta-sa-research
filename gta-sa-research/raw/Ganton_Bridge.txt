The '''Ganton Bridge''' is an overpass featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Description==
It is located in the districts of [[Ganton]] and [[East Los Santos]]. It passes over [[Grove Street (3D Universe)|Grove Street]] and the [[East Beach Freeway Extension]]. The bridge plays an important role in two missions: first in [[House Party]], where Carl must kill some [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] atop the bridge; and second in [[End of the Line]], when Officer [[Frank Tenpenny]] crashes his [[Firetruck (3D Universe)|fire truck]] through the bridge's wall, landing on Grove Street.

==Collectibles and Pickups==
A [[Kilo Tray Ballas]] [[Tags|graffiti tag]] can be found under the bridge, as well as an {{HUDIcons|game=O|casinoconcierge|yellow}} information pickup. This information is the only pickup of its kind in the game.

[[Brass Knuckles]] can be found near the information pickup.
