'''OG Johnson''' is an unseen character mentioned in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]''.

==History==
===Background===
He is an OG (Original Gangster) of the [[Carson Avenue Families]] street gang in [[South Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]].

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto V''===
OG Johnson, along with other unseen CAF members, is briefly mentioned by a CAF gangster in South Los Santos when greeting him while playing as [[Franklin Clinton]]. This character is not relevant to the storyline and his whereabouts are unknown.

==Trivia==
*His name appears to be a reference to the Johnson Brothers ([[Carl Johnson|Carl]] and [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet]]), who led the [[Grove Street Families]] gang from the [[3D Universe]] canon.
**This is one of the many examples of [[Rockstar Games]] referring to 3D Universe characters as "mythical characters from a bygone era" after the series' reboot with the release of ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV]]''.<ref>{{Quote|...the move to HD created a schism in this fictional universe, rendering CJ, Toni and co, as mythical characters from a bygone era.|Interview with [[Dan Houser]] in [https://www.theguardian.com/technology/gamesblog/2012/nov/12/grand-theft-auto-v-preview-gta-5 The Guardian], about the second reboot of the series.}}</ref>
