'''Burning Desire''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[C.R.A.S.H.]] officers [[Frank Tenpenny]], [[Eddie Pulaski]] and [[Jimmy Hernandez]] from the [[Jim's Sticky Ring]] doughnut shop in [[Market (3D Universe)|Market]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Description==
Carl enters the doughnut shop and is spotted by [[Frank Tenpenny]], who tells him to come and sit with them. Tenpenny and Pulaski act with hostility towards Carl, with Tenpenny reminding him that C.R.A.S.H. owns him. Tenpenny then informs Carl of a 'gangbanging, drug-pushing, cop-killing bitch' holed up in a [[Los Santos Vagos House|building]] in [[East Los Santos (3D Universe)|East Los Santos]] that doesn't like them. Tenpenny tells Carl that he wants the gangster dead.

Carl first heads to an alleyway in [[Downtown Los Santos (3D Universe)|Downtown]] to collect some [[Molotov Cocktails|molotov cocktails]] that C.R.A.S.H. had left for him, before heading over to the building in East Los Santos. Carl kills the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]] gangsters surrounding the building and then throws molotov cocktails into five windows around the building. The 'holed up' man, a member of the Los Santos Vagos, runs out of the building. Being badly burnt by the flames, he quickly dies from his injuries.

Carl then hears a girl trapped inside. To rescue her, Carl enters the building and gets to the upstairs bedroom she is trapped inside. Then, using a [[Fire Extinguisher|fire extinguisher]] obtained from the kitchen, he extinguishes the flames blocking their path, helping the girl escape the crumbling building. The girl graciously thanks Carl, and asks him to bring her to her [[Denise Robinson's House|house]]. Once there, she introduces herself as [[Denise Robinson]], and both of them agree to go on a date in the near future.

==Mission Objectives==
*Tenpenny has hidden some '''<span style="color:#4C5C41">molotovs</span>''' in an alleyway in Downtown Los Santos. Pick them up.
*You've got molotovs. Go to the '''<span style="color:#D1BC86">gang house</span>''' and torch it.
*Torch the house. Throw molotovs into the '''<span style="color:#AB3736">5 downstairs windows</span>''' - that'll make it burn.
*There are some '''<span style="color:#4C5C41">molotovs</span>''' nearby. (if the player wastes all molotovs)
*You've trapped a girl inside the burning building. You'll have to save her.
*Take the fire extinguisher upstairs and rescue the '''<span style="color:#484576">girl</span>'''.
*Drive the girl to her house.

==Reward==
There is no monetary reward for completing the mission, but the player can collect [[Money|$]]1,000 inside the burning house; there is [[Money|$]]500 next to the bed in the bedroom where Denise is waiting to be saved, and another [[Money|$]]500 to be found in the living room on the ground floor. The missions [[Doberman]] and [[Gray Imports]] are unlocked. Denise becomes Carl's [[Girlfriends in GTA San Andreas|girlfriend]].

==Trivia==
*The interior of the doughnut shop is branded as [[Rusty Brown's Ring Donuts (3D Universe)|Rusty Brown's Ring Donuts]], even though the exterior of the building seemingly indicates another name, more precisely [[Jim's Sticky Ring]], further enforcing the original concept for Rusty Brown's Ring donuts being in {{SA}}.
*Oddly, when Tenpenny calls out to Carl after he enters the donut shop during the opening cutscene, Carl will mutter "Oh shit!" as if surprised that C.R.A.S.H. was there, despite the fact that Tenpenny called Carl after the mission 'Madd Dogg's Rhymes' and told him to come there so they could talk.
*A [[Police (vehicle)|Police Car]] will always spawn outside the doughnut shop after the opening [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] ends. This vehicle can be stolen without incurring a [[Wanted Level in GTA San Andreas|wanted level]].
**It's possible that the car is left by [[C.R.A.S.H.]] for CJ.
*Even if Carl already has [[Molotov Cocktails|molotov cocktails]] in his inventory before starting the mission, the game will still force the player to collect them from the alleyway.
*While approaching the house, there is a [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]] member standing outside who appears to be talking on a [[Mobile Phone|cell phone]]. Upon closer inspection (using a [[camera]] or [[Sniper Rifle|sniper rifle]]), the gang member is actually holding a [[Pistol (3D Universe)|pistol]] against his ear. 
*If the player has no molotovs by the time they have to torch the house (by wasting them all), another 20 will spawn in front of the house, behind the tree, and 20 at the back of the house. They will keep on respawning if the player loses all of them again.
**Moreover, if the player uses [[Tear Gas|tear gas]] canisters or [[grenades]] in order to smash the windows, they will strangely have the same effect as molotov cocktails, causing a [[fire]]. 
*If the player fails the mission while inside the [[Los Santos Vagos House|apartment]] without dying, Carl can be seen running out of the house.
