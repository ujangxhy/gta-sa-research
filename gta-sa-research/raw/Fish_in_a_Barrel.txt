'''Fish in a Barrel''' is a mission in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]'' given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by the [[Mountain Cloud Boys]] [[San Fierro Triads|Triad]] boss [[Wu Zi Mu]] from his office at [[The Four Dragons Casino]], a casino located along [[The Strip]] in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

Although considered a mission by the game, it is merely a small cutscene.

==Mission==
[[Carl Johnson]] and [[Ran Fa Li]] enter Wu Zi Mu's office at the casino, where Woozie asks them to sign a document. Carl and Ran Fa Li sign it, and Woozie announces that they are now his partners in the Four Dragons Casino. The cutscene ends with the three men raising a toast in celebration.

==Reward==
There is no reward for completing this mission, though the mission [[Freefall]] is unlocked.

==Trivia==
*As with [[The Green Sabre]] (except for mobile versions and ''The Definitive Edition'') and [[King in Exile]], no "Mission Passed" message appears at the end of the mission.
*This cutscene, internally codenamed "Casino 7 Cutscene '''B'''" in the text files, is simply a leftover outro cutscene from a [[Fish in a Barrel (cut mission)|cut mission of the same name]] for the Four Dragons Casino, in which Carl Johnson was intended to escort Ran Fa Li from the airport to the casino and protect him from a [[Mafia]] attack.
*This is the last appearance of Ran Fa Li in the game's storyline and notably the only time he appears without his interpreter.
