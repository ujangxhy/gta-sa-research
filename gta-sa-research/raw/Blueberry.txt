'''Blueberry''' is a medium-sized rural town located in [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Description==
The town of Blueberry is situated near [[Blueberry Acres]] (hence the name) in Red County, [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]] with [[The Panopticon]] to the west, [[Fern Ridge]] to the east and [[Hampton Barns]] to the northeast. The population size of this town is estimated at 1,309. This rural town is the home of the [[FleischBerg]] brewery, whose motto, "Making Life Mean Something Since 1869", stands out prominently underneath its logo. Towards the center of the town, there is a [[Blueberry Safehouse|safehouse]] in an apartment complex which can be bought for [[Money|$]]10,000.

Blueberry is much the same as any other town in Red County with residences and businesses much the same as those in neighboring towns. The town is the industry hub of Red County as it has some of the largest factories in the state. It is connected to a major [[highway]] and serviced by several major roads making it a key stop for travelers making their way through the State of San Andreas. It remains a strong rural community as evidenced by the nearby [[Blueberry Acres]] which along with the [[FleischBerg]] brewery is likely a major employer. Blueberry has the lowest elevation of all towns in [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]]. One of the six girlfriends, [[Helena Wankstein]] can also be found here at the shooting range near the [[Ammu-Nation (3D Universe)|gun shop]].

==Influence==
The town of Blueberry appears to be based on both {{WP|Santa Maria, California|Santa Maria}} and {{WP|Bakersfield, California}}.
