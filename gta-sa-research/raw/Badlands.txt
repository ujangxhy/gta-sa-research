'''Badlands''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[C.R.A.S.H.]] officers [[Frank Tenpenny]], [[Eddie Pulaski]] and [[Jimmy Hernandez]] from a [[Angel Pine Save Point|trailer]] in [[Angel Pine]], [[Whetstone]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Mission==
After the [[The Green Sabre|gunfight]] beneath the [[Mulholland Intersection]] that resulted in [[Sean Johnson|Sweet]] being badly injured and taken into police custody; Carl is kidnapped and taken outside of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]] to begin performing errands for C.R.A.S.H. The first of these tasks is a favor for Tenpenny, who tells Carl to kill an [[The Informant|ex-police officer]] being held by the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] under a witness protection program. The witness, who has compromising information about Tenpenny, is hiding in a [[Mount Chiliad Cabin|remote cabin]] located on [[Mount Chiliad (3D Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. Carl, disarmed by the police, and carrying only a [[camera]], is forced to make his way up Mount Chiliad. He then flushes the witness out of the cabin, and chases him down Mount Chiliad as he attempts to flee in a [[Washington]]. After Carl kills the witness, he photographs the evidence that he will no longer be talking, and delivers it to a trailer in Angel Pine. If the witness makes it to the road at the base of the mountain, he will continue north until reaching the road in south [[Foster Valley]], after which he will continue randomly until killed by Carl.

After completing the mission in Angel Pine, Carl will receive two phone calls in the first succession. The first phone call is from [[Cesar Vialpando|Cesar]], who suggests Carl to locate an [[Catalina|unknown person]] who will protect him. The second is from Sweet, now held captive in a prison hospital while receiving treatment for his gunshot wounds.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Kill the '''[[The Informant|<span style="color:#D1BC86">witness</span>]]'''! Bring back proof.
*Make your way up the '''[[Mount Chiliad (3D Universe)|<span style="color:#D1BC86">mountain</span>]]''' to find that snitch!
*He's inside, smoke him out!
*Watch out for the feds!
*Remember, you need a photo of him in one piece!
*Photograph the '''<span style="color:#AB3736">body</span>'''.
*Take the [[camera]] to the '''[[Angel Pine Save Point|<span style="color:#D1BC86">drop</span>]]'''.

==Deaths==
*[[The Informant]] – Killed by Carl Johnson on the orders of Frank Tenpenny.
*[[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]] guards (optional) – Can be killed by Carl for getting in his way of killing the witness.

==Reward==
There is no reward for completing this mission, though the mission [[First Date (GTA San Andreas)|First Date]] is unlocked.

==Trivia==
*The Washington that the witness drives has a unique [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] [[Vehicle Features|license plate]] that reads "ASSMAN".
*If the player manages to destroy the Washington before the witness enters it, he will remain in the cabin and not move.
*If the player lost the infinite camera received after The Green Sabre, they will receive a new one at the start of this mission.
*If the player somehow loses the camera during this mission, the mission will continue as normal until the player successfully kills the target, when the game will instruct the player to pick up a new one from the drop point.
*If the player headshots the informant with weapons that can decapitate, such as the [[Shotgun (3D Universe)|Shotgun]], [[Sawed-Off Shotgun]], [[Katana]], or the [[Pistol .50|Desert Eagle]], it will cause the mission to fail as the body can't be identified.
*FBI agents in this mission are different from the normal ones. They have much lower health, carry [[Micro SMG (HD Universe)|Micro SMGs]] instead of [[SMG (3D Universe)|SMGs]] and don't try to [[Busted|arrest]] the player if he has a [[Wanted Level in GTA San Andreas|wanted level]]. Also, they will become neutral after the witness escapes in the Washington.
*The [[Sanchez]] used by Carl is all-proof. There is a specific method of obtaining the unique vehicle; once the chase sequence begins, the player must take the bike to the beach near the bottom of the mountain. The Sanchez must be driven deep into the water. Once the player dismounts and fails the mission, they can now push or ride the Sanchez out of the water, after which it can be stored in a garage.
