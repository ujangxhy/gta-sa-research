'''The Da Nang Thang''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by the [[Mountain Cloud Boys]] [[San Fierro Triads|Triad]] boss [[Wu Zi Mu]] from his [[Wu Zi Mu's Apartment|apartment]] in [[Chinatown (GTA San Andreas)|Chinatown]], [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Mission==
Carl bumps into Woozie just as he is about to leave his apartment. Suddenly, [[Little Lion]], a Triad gang member, calls Woozie and informs him of the [[Da Nang Boys]] having arrived on a [[Da Nang Boys Freighter|freighter ship]] in the [[San Fierro Bay]] and having brought along some [[Vietnam]]ese refugees who were locked in a [[Shipping Containers|container]].

Carl rushes to the apartment's roof and meets Little Lion, who is about to fly towards the ship with his [[Maverick (3D Universe)|Maverick]]. Carl enters the helicopter and the two fly over to the ship in the middle of the bay. Carl, prepared for the attack, starts killing every Da Nang Boy he spots with a [[minigun]] with infinite ammunition and after a brief moment, one of the Da Nang Boys manages to shoot down the helicopter with a [[Rocket Launcher (3D Universe)|rocket launcher]]. Little Lion is killed while CJ survives the attack, but loses all his weapons, and some of his health, leaving him with only a [[knife]].

Carl boards the ship and kills several Da Nang Boys, continuing his way around until he finds the prisoners and frees them. One of the refugees tells him where [[The Snakehead]], the Da Nang Boys' leader, is located in ship. Carl makes his way to the Snakehead, killing all enemies in the way. During the fight, Carl kills the Snakehead and then meets up with the refugees. They lower some [[Dinghy (3D Universe)|Dinghies]] into the [[water]] and escape after gratefully thanking Carl for everything he did, leaving Carl alone on a Dinghy.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Swim over to the '''<span style="color:#D1BC86">freighter</span>'''.
*Make your way down into the '''<span style="color:#D1BC86">hull</span>''' of the ship. Be quiet for as long as possible!
*Take out the refugees' '''<span style="color:#AB3736">guard</span>'''.
*Shoot the '''<span style="color:#4C5C41">padlock</span>''' on the front of the refugees' container.
*Leave no one alive on the bridge!
*Kill The Snakehead.
*Go back and meet the '''<span style="color:#484576">refugees</span>'''. They will lower some dinghies into the water.

==Deaths==
*[[Little Lion]] - Killed by the [[Da Nang Boys]] after his helicopter is shot down with a [[Rocket Launcher (3D Universe)|rocket launcher]].
*[[The Snakehead]] - Killed by [[Carl Johnson]] on orders from [[Wu Zi Mu]].
*Numerous members of the Da Nang Boys - Killed by Carl Johnson for getting in his way of rescuing the refugees, as well as to leave no one alive on the freighter ship.

==Reward==
The rewards for this mission are [[Money|$]]15,000 and increased [[respect]]. If the mission [[Toreno's Last Flight]] has been completed, the mission [[Yay Ka-Boom-Boom]] is unlocked.

==Trivia==
===General===
*The sword fight in the mission is similar to the mission [[Cash in Kazuki's Chips]] in {{LCS}}, in which [[Toni Cipriani|Toni]] will be prompted to engage the enemy in the same way and is free to choose any weapon he wants. However, Toni's fight is harder due to the presence of heavily armed guards that will attack Toni when he pulls out a firearm and/or [[Kazuki Kasen|Kazuki]] is low on [[health]].

===''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
*Although [[Wu Zi Mu|Woozie]] is informed that the [[Da Nang Boys]] have just arrived in [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] on a [[Da Nang Boys Freighter|container ship]], the ship has been anchored in the [[San Fierro Bay|bay]] since the start of the game due to it being a static object.
*Regardless of how accurate the player is with the [[minigun]], the gangster with the [[Rocket Launcher (3D Universe)|rocket launcher]] is invincible; therefore, the [[Maverick (3D Universe)|helicopter]] will always be shot down and [[Carl Johnson|Carl]] will lose his weapons collection.
**Even if the player shoots the missile, or the missile misses the helicopter, the helicopter will still be destroyed.
**In addition, the helicopter is actually sunken, but not destroyed. It is invincible after the crash. It can be obtained by dropping down to the seabed, and then pushing it to the shore either with CJ's body or with the [[Sawed-Off Shotgun]]. After that, it can be entered, flown to a [[Garages|garage]] and stored. It can be stored in the garages of the [[Doherty Safehouse]] and [[Hashbury Safehouse]] at this point in the game. A demonstration of this can be seen {{URL|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}jDhqv2QSu38|here}}. 
*Even if the player is [[Fist|unarmed]] or has a different melee weapon, Carl will automatically receive a [[knife]] after the helicopter is shot down.
*On rare occasions, due to the ship's height, a [[plane]] may crash into it, resulting in the death of [[The Snakehead]] and his guards. This causes Carl to automatically meet up with the refugees. This can also cause a softlock as a plane might crash into the hull, destroying boxes in the process. If CJ is too fat/not muscular enough, they might not be able to climb out until being starved by waiting for several in-game hours.
*During this mission, it is impossible to get a [[Wanted Level in GTA San Andreas|wanted level]]. Accessing [[Impound Garage|impound garages]] will cause nearby officers to shoot the player, but no wanted level will be attained. Accessing the [[Easter Basin Naval Station]] can cause [[FBI Rancher]]s to spawn on the road, but not chase the player.
**In addition, one can access the locked regions of [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]] and [[Area 69 (3D Universe)|Area 69]] freely. However, if the player flies an aircraft over their airspace, the [[Surface-to-Air Missile|SAM]] sites will fire on the player regardless of the wanted level and can [[Wasted|waste]] them.
*After the mission has been completed, the [[West Coast Talk Radio (3D Universe)|WCTR]] [[Lianne Forget|news reporter]] reports on the "mystery of a container ship found deserted and full of bodies", as a subtle reference to the aftermath of this mission. The broadcast can be first heard when the player is driving to the location of the contraband helicopter in the mission [[Interdiction]].
