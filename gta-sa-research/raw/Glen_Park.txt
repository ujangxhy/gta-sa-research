}}'''Glen Park''' is a neighborhood of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], that appears in {{SA}}.

==Description==
The neighborhood is located to the north-east of the city center of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], in ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''. It has [[Las Colinas]] to the northeast, [[Idlewood]] to the south, [[Jefferson (neighborhood)|Jefferson]] to the east, and [[Downtown Los Santos (3D Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]] to the west.

The district is quite affluent and middle-class, even though there are severe problems with gangs and drug trafficking. It consists of many bungalows, apartments, a public park with surrounding palm trees, a large pond in the centre, and an overpass bridge. The nearby [[County General Hospital]] can also be found despite being located in the official zone of [[Jefferson (neighborhood)|Jefferson]].

Glen Park is controlled by the [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] in the early part of [[1992]], and is close to other Ballas controlled districts, namely Jefferson and [[Idlewood]]. It is later controlled by the [[Grove Street Families]]. Besides the two aforementioned districts, nearby areas include [[Downtown Los Santos (3D Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]], the [[Mulholland Intersection]] and the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]]-controlled [[Las Colinas]]. There are two known residents: [[B Dup|Mark "B Dup" Wayne]] and [[Big Bear|Barry "Big Bear" Thorne]], who moved to Glen Park from [[Ganton]]. The area has a diverse multicultural population.

==Influence==
Glen Park may have taken inspiration from real-life {{WP|MacArthur Park}} and {{WP|Echo Park, Los Angeles|Echo Park}} in {{WP|Los Angeles}}. The bridge over the lake is based on {{WP|Wilshire Boulevard}} bisecting MacArthur Park's lake, however, in reality the northern part of the lake was drained at some point after completion of the boulevard.<ref>https://laist.com/news/climate-environment/los-angeles-macarthur-park-lake-reconnect-wilshire</ref><ref>https://www.plannersnetwork.org/2008/07/playing-out-democracy-in-macarthur-park-spatial-struggles-in-the-everyday-use-of-public-space-2/</ref> In the game, the park is depicted as being contested between African-American gangs, while in reality there was mainly Hispanic gang presence in the area in the 80s and 90s.<ref>https://ericbrightwell.com/2018/01/28/southland-parks-visiting-macarthur-park/</ref>

==History==
===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
{{Spoiler}}
Glen Park in the early part of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'' is controlled by the [[Kilo Tray Ballas]], although they eventually lose the area to the Grove Street Families (during the mission [[Doberman]]). However, the district falls back into the hands of the Ballas, when [[Carl Johnson]] and [[Sean Johnson|Sean "Sweet" Johnson]] lose influence over the Grove Street Families. Upon Sweet Johnson's parole, the GSF retake Glen Park during a turf war in a [[Beat Down on B Dup|later mission]].
