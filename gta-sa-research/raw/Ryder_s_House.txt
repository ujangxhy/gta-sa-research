'''Ryder's House''' is located on [[Grove Street (3D Universe)|Grove Street]] in [[Ganton]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], between the [[Johnson House]] and the Grove Street overpass.

==Description==
The property consists of a bungalow with a driveway and back garden. There is Ryder's brown [[Picador]] parked in the driveway. The interior of the house is featured briefly during mission [[cutscenes]], with only the kitchen and the living room visible. In the living room, a boxart of {{VC}} can be seen. In the back garden, a [[shovel]] can be found, along with several holes which were dug by Ryder during the mission Home Invasion whilst looking for [[drugs]] he buried in the garden.

Following Ryder's [[Pier 69 (mission)|death]], the house remains unoccupied for the rest of the game, though the Picador can still be found parked in the driveway.
