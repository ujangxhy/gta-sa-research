'''Obey''' is a [[Germany|German]] luxury automotive manufacturer appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

==Description==
In {{VO}}, Obey is featured on advertisements around [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], and it is implied that they are direct competitors with [[Übermacht]].

==Influence==
The company is the in-game counterpart of {{WP|Audi}}. The logo is based on the four interlinked rings of the Audi logo, although modified. The name "Obey" is an obvious play on "Audi", as Audi means the imperative "hear" in Latin, and it has a similar meaning with obey.
