'''Vertical Bird''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by government agent [[Mike Toreno]] from [[Madd Dogg's Crib]] in [[Mulholland]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Mission==
After [[A Home in the Hills|reclaiming]] [[Madd Dogg (3D Universe)|Madd Dogg's]] mansion from the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]], Carl returns to the mansion, where he listens to Madd Dogg performing in his recording studio with [[Kent Paul]] and [[Maccer]]. All of a sudden, the audio is cut off and Toreno's voice is heard on the speakers, requesting that Carl meet him outside the mansion. Carl leaves Paul, Maccer and Dogg in the studio and enters Toreno's [[Washington]] outside.

Toreno drives Carl to the northern shore of [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]]. Requesting another job to be performed for him in exchange for [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet]]'s release from prison, Toreno demands that Carl sneak inside an [[LHD-069|amphibious assault ship]] in the [[Easter Basin Naval Station]] to steal a [[Hydra]] VTOL jet to destroy a flotilla of spy ships. Carl hops in a [[Squalo (3D Universe)|Squalo]] speedboat containing a [[knife]] and a [[Silenced 9mm|silenced pistol]], and heads for the naval base. Carl enters the ship through the rear, where he has the option to either blast his way in, or sneak inside the ship using his equipment. Regardless, Carl disables the ship's [[Surface-to-Air Missile|S.A.M.]] sites and steals one of the Hydras.

Shortly afterwards, a group of Hydras are dispatched to shoot down Carl's aircraft. Toreno tells Carl not to worry about it, stating that a shootdown would cause an international incident. Carl destroys the pursuing aircraft and flies towards the [[Sherman Reservoir]] where he uses the Hydra's weaponry to destroy the [[Tropic]] spy ships, as Toreno requested. With his task completed, an infuriated Carl now asks Toreno what to do with the jet, but Toreno tells Carl that it is his problem. Carl flies the aircraft back to the [[Verdant Meadows Airfield|Verdant Meadows airfield]], where he taxis the jet into one of the hangars, completing the mission.

==Trivia==
===General===
*[[Mike Toreno|Toreno]] reassuring [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] that he should be fine because he is not a [[United Kingdom|British]] tank is likely a reference to one particular friendly fire incident during the 1990–1991 {{WP|Gulf War}}, when two British {{WP|FV510 Warrior}} tracked armoured vehicles, mistaken for [[Iraq]]i {{WP|T-54/T-55|T-55 tanks}}, were destroyed by {{WP|AGM-65 Maverick}} missiles launched from two [[United States of America|American]] {{WP|Fairchild Republic A-10 Thunderbolt II|A-10 Thunderbolt II}} ground attack aircraft.

===''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
*This is the only storyline mission that takes place across each of the three major areas in the [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|State of San Andreas]]. It starts at [[Madd Dogg's Crib]] ([[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]]), Carl steals the [[Hydra]] from the [[Easter Basin Naval Station]] ([[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]]), destroys [[Tropic|spy ships]] in [[Tierra Robada]] and lands the jet at [[Verdant Meadows Airfield|Verdant Meadows]] (near [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]]).
**The only mission that covers more distance is the second [[Beat the Cock!]] triathlon. It starts in [[Palomino Creek]], traverses the countryside and requires the player to enter San Fierro, [[Bayside]] and Las Venturas properly, the latter two requiring a passage through the desert.
*The default radio station of the [[Washington]] is [[West Coast Talk Radio (3D Universe)|WCTR]], but when Carl and Toreno enter Toreno's car, [[K-Rose]] is playing.
*In the beta, Carl would have to drive all the way to the [[Squalo (3D Universe)|boat]], as depicted by unused mission failure messages (i.e. "Toreno's car is destroyed!").
*The mission is scripted to take place at night and have stormy [[weather]].
*If the [[Launch|patrol boat]] that enters the carrier is destroyed from afar (with a [[minigun]] or an [[Rocket Launcher (3D Universe)|RPG]]), the game will crash as soon as the player sneaks aboard.
*On the aircraft carrier, three jets can be found initially. A fourth one lands later on, while one of the initial three departs. Destroying one of the remaining jets will lead to two Hydras pursuing CJ after stealing a Hydra, while destroying two will lead to just one Hydra pursuing CJ.
*The [[Surface-to-Air Missile|S.A.M.]] sites on the aircraft carrier are mission-exclusive, and will remain inactive out of the mission, regardless of whether they were deactivated or not by the player during the mission.
