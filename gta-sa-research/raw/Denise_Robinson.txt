'''Denise Robinson''' is a minor character in {{SA}}. She can become one of [[Carl Johnson|Carl's]] potential [[Girlfriends in GTA San Andreas|girlfriends]].

She is voiced by [[Heather Alicia Simms]].

==History==
===Background===
Denise grew up in [[Ganton Courts]]{{fact}} in [[Grove Street (3D Universe)|Grove Street]], and some time in her life, became a supporter of the [[Grove Street Families]]. She later moved into a [[Denise Robinson's House|house]] a block away from her childhood home. At an unknown point in her life, she became pregnant and gave birth to three children.{{Ref|name=kids}}

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Denise first encounters CJ during the mission [[Burning Desire]], where CJ, on the orders of officer [[Frank Tenpenny]], sets [[fire]] to a [[Los Santos Vagos House|house]] owned by [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]] members. Denise, who was inside the upper bedroom of the house, screams for help, causing CJ to venture into the burning building to rescue her. During the car journey home, Denise recognizes CJ as being [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet's]] brother, and asks him why he moved away to [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]], to which CJ denies her belief that he "ran away from home". She also informs CJ of having been in Vagos territory as she likes to "live dangerously", indicating that she could work as a [[Prostitutes|prostitute]].

==Dates==
{{Main|Girlfriends in GTA San Andreas}}
After completing the mission Burning Desire, Denise can be found at her house in [[Ganton]] '''between 16:00 and 06:00''' (4:00 PM and 6:00 AM). Unlike other girlfriends, Denise has no requirements in order to date her.

===Food Date===
Denise prefers to go to either [[Cluckin' Bell (3D Universe)|Cluckin' Bell]], [[The Well Stacked Pizza Co. (3D Universe)|The Well Stacked Pizza Co.]] or at [[Ten Green Bottles]], a bar just a small drive northwest of her home. Although all three of these establishments are only short drives away from each other, Ten Green Bottles is the easiest to drive to, as it is only a block away from her home. Diners, however, are her least favorite choice.

===Dancing Date===
Denise prefers to go to [[Alhambra]], a club on the edge of [[Idlewood]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]]. The player can also take her here on dinner dates, as the venue features a bar.

===Driving Date===
Denise likes the areas surrounding her home, such as Ganton and Idlewood, and does not mind how fast or slow Carl drives. She dislikes driving around affluent districts of the city, as well as rural areas such as [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]] or [[Flint County (3D Universe)|Flint County]].

===Alternative date===
The alternative date for Denise is to do a [[Drive-By Shooting|drive-by]] on other gangs in Los Santos, whether it be at cars or a group of people. Allowing Denise to do a drive-by will normally make her happy and it will not fail the original date she sets at the start.

The alternative date can be completed instead of performing the original intended dates, such as dancing. If the player was meant to go on a driving date, they can see that the "fun" meter will fill up when Denise performs a drive-by.

If Denise asks to do a drive-by after the player has taken over 100% of the gang territories in Los Santos, she won't shoot at any Grove Street gang members. However, if CJ enters the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Aztecas]] territory, she will ask to do a drive-by.

===Coffee===
Denise should start inviting Carl in for "coffee" after dates when the relationship with her is at about 40%.

==Rewards==
Denise rewards Carl when the relationship reaches 50% by giving him the keys to her dark green [[Hustler]], which is parked outside her house during the times she is available, although it will not spawn if she is not home. At 100%, Denise gives Carl the [[Pimp Suit]].

*There is a {{VC}} poster inside of Denise's house, although this can only be seen through the [[Modifications/Hot Coffee|Hot Coffee]] mod or by using exploits inside [[Interiors in GTA San Andreas|interiors]].
*Her license plate on the green [[Hustler]] reads "HOMEGIRL", referencing her ghetto lifestyle.
*It is possible that Denise is a [[Prostitutes|prostitute]], as she was seen with the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]], alongside the fact she gives CJ the [[Pimp Suit]] once he reaches 100% progress with her and the fact she had three children that she gave away (unplanned pregnancies). It is also suggested by her car which is a "[[Hustler]]", which is also a slang term for [[pimp]].
*She is the easiest girlfriend to date, as she is not picky and the establishments are near, in comparison to [[Helena Wankstein]], who lives at a [[Helena Wankstein's Farm|ranch]] far away from any establishment. But she is also the clingiest one, since phone calls and progress decrease due to neglect are much more frequent with her than with any other girlfriend.
*If Denise dies during a date, she will drop the [[Micro SMG (3D Universe)|Micro SMG]] utilized to [[Drive-By Shooting|drive-by]] enemy gangs. This makes her the only girlfriend in the game who drops a weapon when killed.
*Sometimes during the game, if the player ever listens to [[Radio Los Santos (3D Universe)|Radio Los Santos]], they might hear her asking Carl to see her because she's desperate. The [[Julio G|DJ]] then complains that shout-outs like those should end up on [[Lonely Hearts]], and not on there. This happens even if Denise is dead or dumped.
*After completing [[The Green Sabre]], at which point Carl is based for a time outside of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], he no longer has any obligations towards Denise: he will not receive nagging phone calls, nor will his progress decrease, as long as he stays outside of Los Santos. If he re-enters the city (except during a mission), the progress bar will reappear (and go up and down accordingly) and Denise will attempt to phone him again. This also applies when Carl re-establishes his base in Los Santos near the end of the game (dating Denise during the [[Los Santos Riots|riots]] is possible, but dangerous to both). Carl has the option to continue dating her, but there is no storyline-related obligation to do so.
*There are some [[pedestrians]] with the same model as Denise in the mission [[A Home in the Hills]]. Killing them won't affect the relationship with Denise, however.
