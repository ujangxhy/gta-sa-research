'''El Corona''' is a neighborhood of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Description==
The neighborhood is a working-class residential district slightly to the south-east of the city centre of Los Santos. Surrounding El Corona are the districts of [[Little Mexico]], [[Verdant Bluffs]], [[Idlewood]] and [[Willowfield]]. Just south is the [[Los Santos International Airport (3D Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]].

El Corona, often called the "Southside", is the main stronghold for the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] gang, and there are five known residents, [[Cesar Vialpando]], [[Gal]], [[Hazer]], [[Sunny]] and [[Kendl Johnson]] (who moves to El Corona to live with Cesar Vialpando).

El Corona can be described as a gang-ridden district with a high crime rate, as evidenced by the ominous gang presence and graffiti. The area primarily contains a large housing project known as the [[El Corona Village]], some stores, businesses, and numerous small, single-story houses. El Corona is primarily a Mexican/Hispanic district.

==Influence==
The area's character, appearance, and proximity to [[Los Santos International Airport (3D Universe)|LSX]] and [[Idlewood]] seem to resemble {{WP|Lennox, California}}. El Corona also seems to be based on {{WP|Pico-Union, Los Angeles|Pico-Union}}, a neighborhood in Los Angeles with relative proximity to Downtown, and a largely Salvadorean and Mexican population. However, the nearby projects resemble the Dana Strand Village housing project in {{WP|Wilmington, Los Angeles|Wilmington}} along with the railway line overpass with the multi-colored stairs which can be found in {{WP|Watts, Los Angeles|Watts}}.

==History==
===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
{{Spoiler}}
El Corona is a stronghold for the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] and the home of Cesar Vialpando, and has (prior to the events of ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]'') been attacked by the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]]. [[Carl Johnson]] is first introduced to El Corona (in a mission) during the [[High Stakes, Low-Rider]] mission, where Carl meets Cesar at his home. After Cesar Vialpando leaves Los Santos, El Corona falls into the control of the Los Santos Vagos.

Upon his return, Cesar and his gang begin to kill Vagos gang members in El Corona, and eventually ask Carl Johnson for help. Carl, Cesar, Gal, Hazer and Sunny [[Los Desperados|kill]] many Vagos gang members, and win the district back for the Varrios Los Aztecas. The area, already [[Los Santos Riots|rioting]] following the acquittal of [[Frank Tenpenny]], continues to riot until Tenpenny's [[End of the Line|death]], at which time discipline is restored to El Corona and the rest of Los Santos.
