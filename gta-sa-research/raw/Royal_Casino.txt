}}The '''Royal Casino''' is a [[casino]] located along [[The Strip]] in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]] that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]''.

==Description==
Located beside the casino are [[The Camel's Toe]] and [[Caligula's Palace]]. It is not accessible, but it has the [[Multiplayer]] icon underneath the roof in [[PlayStation 2|PS2]] and [[Xbox (console)|Xbox]] versions.

==Influence==
The casino is based on {{WP|Casino Royale & Hotel}}, which is now known as the Best Western Plus Casino Royale in {{WP|Las Vegas, Nevada}}.

==Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''==
In the [[Madd Dogg (mission)|mission Madd Dogg]], this is the casino where [[Carl Johnson|Carl]] saves [[Madd Dogg (3D Universe)|Madd Dogg]] from falling to his death.
