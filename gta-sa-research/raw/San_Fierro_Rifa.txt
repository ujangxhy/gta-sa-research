The '''San Fierro Rifa''' (or Rifa) is a [[Mexico|Mexican]] gang in {{SA}}, operating in [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]]. It is led by [[T-Bone Mendez]].

==Description==
===Relationships with other gangs===
The Rifa had a strong dislike towards other Mexican gangs in [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], until they joined the narcotics business with [[Big Smoke]] and linked up with the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]].

The Rifa and Vagos often deal with each other in narcotics as seen in the mission [[Wrong Side of the Tracks]]. They are also on unfriendly terms with the other San Fierro gangs {{nd}} the [[China|Chinese]] [[San Fierro Triads]] and the [[Vietnam]]ese [[Da Nang Boys]], though confrontations of these gangs are rare, as their territories are far away from each other. Moreover, the focus of the Rifa is also not based on a gang war with the other San Fierro gangs, but on the narcotics business.

They are the least threatening and violent on the streets: when the player (or any other gang) attacks them, they usually run. Nearby Rifa will question Carl on his gang-banging status. If the player chooses to respond positively {{nd}} the Rifa will sometimes (but not always) attack as opposed to the Los Santos gangs who will almost always attack. They are never armed with any weapons on the streets, but will draw a [[Pistol (3D Universe)|pistol]] if they are provoked with [[Melee Weapons|melee weapons]], by answering positively or when crashing into their cars (although sometimes they will ram back instead). In general, the Rifa are completely neutral to CJ (except for missions), even after Mendez's death.

===Physical Appearance===
Rifa members nearly have the same turquoise color bandannas that [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] wear (the Rifa colors are slightly more vibrant), but are not to be mistaken as a northern set of the gang, as they do not like each other. Also, Rifa members are easily recognizable because of their baggy slacks. They have three different skins that will drive and patrol around their territories:

*The first member wears a white fedora, a turquoise bandanna, a white beater, black slacks, suspenders, black shoes and has tattoos.
*The second member wears sunglasses, a turquoise bandanna, a black vest, a cross necklace, blue slacks, brown shoes and has tattoos.
*The last member wears a collared blue T-shirt, a turquoise bandanna, black slacks, suspenders, black shoes and also has tattoos.

The San Fierro Rifa gang members are seen driving [[Stallion]]s, [[Sabre]]s, and [[Blade (car)|Blades]] and listen to the radio station [[K-JAH West]].

==Influence==
The San Fierro Rifa is based on the real life gang, the {{WP|Norteños}}. In reality, however, the Nortenos use red as their dominant color, but like the Rifa, they are the primary Mexican-American criminal group in {{WP|San Francisco}}, which San Fierro is based on.

==Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''==
The gang established drug trade relations with Big Smoke, the [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]], Los Santos Vagos and [[Russian Mafia (3D Universe)|Russian Mafia]] after Big Smoke's [[The Green Sabre|betrayal]].

Despite the death of their leader during the events of the game, the gang remains in existence in their turfs throughout San Fierro, although following the destruction of the [[Loco Syndicate]] and their [[Loco Syndicate Crack Factory|crack lab]], it is unlikely they are involved in the drugs trade any further, as there is nobody else to supply them.

They make their final appearance in [[End of the Line]] where they were hired by Big Smoke for protection purposes of his [[Big Smoke's Crack Palace|crack empire]] in collaboration with the Ballas, Los Santos Vagos, and Russian Mafia. Ultimately, the Rifas, alongside the other gangs, are defeated.
