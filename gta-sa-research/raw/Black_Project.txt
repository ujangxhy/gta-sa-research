'''Black Project''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by pot-growing hippie [[The Truth]] from the [[Verdant Meadows Airfield]] in [[Bone County (3D Universe)|Bone County]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Description==
Carl arrives at the airfield and is welcomed by [[The Truth]], exercising on the top of an aircraft wreck. The Truth then berates Carl for having willfully worked for a [[Mike Toreno|government agent]]. He then tells Carl about a [[Area 69 (3D Universe)|secret military research area]], where he believes a "black project" is being developed. The Truth drops Carl off at the boundaries of the base before leaving.

Carl must sneak into the underground area, by using the control tower to open the blast doors and get inside. However, he has to do so before sunrise, and the area is heavily guarded with several soldiers patrolling the area, as well as many searchlights. If Carl manages to get into the control tower, he has a limited amount of time to reach the blast doors before they're closed again. If Carl stays in a searchlight for too long or destroys three of the searchlights, the blast doors will be isolated from the main system, forcing him to find another way to get inside, namely through a vent.

Once inside, whether through the blast doors or the vent, the alarm goes off, and Carl is forced to kill every soldier in the way as he tries to reach a research lab for the black project. Upon arriving at the lab, a scientist gives Carl his keycard, which he uses to gain access to the launch bay, where the project, which turns out to be a [[Jetpack]], is lying at the bottom. Carl steals it and flies towards [[Arco del Oeste]], where The Truth is waiting in his [[Camper (3D Universe)#Variants|van]]. The Truth takes the jetpack and drives off, leaving Carl all alone in the desert as he asks The Truth if he could drive him back into town.

*Deliver the jetpack to a drop off point deep in the canyons

==Reward==
There is no [[Money|monetary]] reward for this mission. A few minutes after completing the mission, The Truth will call Carl, which unlocks the mission [[Green Goo]]. The [[Desert Eagle]] is now available to buy from the [[Ammu-Nation (3D Universe)|Ammu-Nation]] for [[Money|$]]1,200.

==Trivia==
===General===
*When inside the [[Area 69 (3D Universe)|Area 69]] research area, various announcements tell the employees a variety of things including (but not limited to): "downloading pornography is strictly prohibited", "anyone caught stealing alien technology will not be invited to the next staff night out", and that "running with scissors is dangerous and not permitted". These announcements continue even after security notifies everyone of the breaches.
**The announcement made about running with scissors not being permitted is a possible reference to the video game company {{WP|Running with Scissors (company)|Running with Scissors}}, best known for its ''Postal'' games, which are considered by some to be "[[GTA Clone]]s".
*"[[General Mills]]" is likely a reference to a well-known {{WP|General Mills|food manufacturing company}} in the [[United States of America|US]], best known for cereals such as {{WP|Cheerios}}.
*The scientists in the base all have [[Germany|German]]/[[Europe]]an accents. This could be a reference to {{WP|Operation Paperclip}}, in which the {{WP|Federal government of the United States|U.S. Government}} employed 1,500 ex-Nazi scientists between 1945 and 1959 to prevent their knowledge from falling into Soviet hands.
*When navigating through the hallways near the scientist who gives Carl the keycard, the player can spot a [[crowbar]] on a table in a lab. This is a reference to {{WC|sectorw|Valve Corporation|Valve}}'s 1998 FPS ''{{WC|sectorw|Half-Life}}'', where the protagonist is a {{WC|sectorw|Gordon Freeman|scientist}} escaping an alien-filled {{WC|sectorw|Black Mesa|underground lab}} with a crowbar as his melee weapon of choice.
*In {{TDE}}, sneaking into the underground base without setting off the alarm will earn the player the "[[They Can't Stop All of Us]]" achievement.
**The achievement name is a reference to the {{WP|Storm Area 51}} {{WP|Facebook}} event.

===''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
*If [[Carl Johnson|CJ]]'s [[Statistics#Fat|fat]] is above 60% when attempting to start this mission, an alternate [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] will play in which [[The Truth]] calls Carl a "fat ass" and then tells him to lose some weight beforehand, most likely to make it easier for the [[Jetpack]] to lift him. A similar thing also happens in [[Green Goo]].
*The ability to remove the Jetpack once equipped is disabled during the mission. This is also the case in the mission Green Goo. However, it can be removed by using an exploit; entering a [[crane]], the closest one being at [[Hunter Quarry]]. However, the player will remain unable to use any vehicles until they purchase a product from a [[Vending Machines|vending machine]]. A video showing the crane at [[The Clown's Pocket]] being used can be seen {{URL|https://www.youtube.com/watch?v{{=}}Y931sZXa80M|here}}.
*There is an area where the player can jump out of bounds on the way to getting the Jetpack that will then teleport them to the room where they leave the base.
*The alarm in the [[Personal Computer|PC]] and original [[Xbox (console)|Xbox]] versions uses a unique sound, which is slower compared to other versions of the game. The alarm from the mobile versions and remastered [[Xbox 360]] version of the game is similar to the PC version, though it is much faster, while the [[PlayStation 2|PS2]] version reuses the alarm sound from [[Vertical Bird]], which is much faster.
*Before the ending cutscene starts, if CJ kills [[The Truth|Truth]] inside his [[Camper (3D Universe)#Variants|Mothership]] while done stealing the jetpack and entering the red marker in a few moments the cutscene and its mission will softlock, making impossible to end the mission since the cutscene is unskippable.
