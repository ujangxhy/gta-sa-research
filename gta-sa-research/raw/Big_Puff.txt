'''Big Puff''' is a hand dryer manufacturer that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto III''.

==Description==
A hand dryer can be found inside the bathroom in [[Belleville Park in [[Staunton Island]]. In both appearances, a [[Blobs]] liquid soap dispenser appears next to the hand dryer.
