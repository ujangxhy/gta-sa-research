'''Avery Carrington''' is a main character in {{VC}} and a minor [[antagonist]] in {{LCS}}.

He was voiced by the late [[Burt Reynolds]].

==History==
===Background===
Avery Carrington was born and raised in {{WP|Texas}} and later moved to [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]], where he became a successful property magnate and real estate mogul willing to use any tactic to earn profits, including gang warfare, arson, intimidation, murder and demolishing the opponents' buildings. Avery is heavily influenced by his father and takes on a protégé, [[Donald Love]], passing on his knowledge, which Love would later [[Waka-Gashira Wipeout!|use]] to his own [[Panlantic Land Grab|advantage]]. However, for some reason, Avery seems to dislike Donald.

He owns the [[Shady Acres]] company, whose advertisement can be heard on the in-game radio. He may also have been involved with the company [[Avery Construction]], a property development company working in [[Blueberry]], [[Red County (3D Universe)|Red County]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City''===
In [[1986]], Avery is financing the [[Avery Construction Site|construction of a building]] in [[Vice Point]] and is one of Vice City's social elite, attending [[Juan Cortez]]' [[The Party|party]]. However, he faces problems with the [[Spand Express]] company, who refuse to sell their land. [[Ken Rosenberg]], a lawyer, introduces Carrington to [[Tommy Vercetti]], who helps to drive Spand Express out of their land by causing a [[Riot (GTA Vice City)|riot]] in their yard and destroying three [[Spand Express (truck)|Spand Express trucks]].

Carrington, impressed with Vercetti's abilities, later hires him to [[Four Iron|murder]] a [[Unnamed Property Developer|man]] who refused to sell some land to Carrington, and to [[Demolition Man|structurally weaken]] a construction site located opposite his own to allow Carrington to buy it at a cheaper price. Avery continues with his business before asking Vercetti for help again, this time having him [[Two Bit Hit|initiate]] a [[Cuban-Haitan War|gang war]] between the [[Cubans]] and the [[Haitians]] in order to drive down property prices. He later visits Vercetti at his [[Vercetti Estate|new mansion]] and gives him [[Shakedown (GTA Vice City)|advice]] on how to build his criminal empire following the [[Rub Out|death]] of drug baron [[Ricardo Diaz]].

===After the events of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City''===
[[File:AveryConstruction-GTASA-advert.jpg|right|thumb|Avery Construction in Blueberry, Red County, San Andreas.]]
Avery Carrington continued to expand his business, appearing on a billboard in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]] in [[1992]].

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories''===
{{Quote|Avery. Oh, Avery! How you've aged since we last met... you used to feed me such pearls of wisdom... and soon I shall dine again!... My God! He's wearing a wig!|[[Donald Love]] in [[Morgue Party Resurrection]].|sound=MorguePartyResurrection-GTALCS-Quote-AveryCarrington.ogg}}
In [[1998]], Avery sends an e-mail to [[Ned Burner]], where he says that [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]]'s mayor [[Roger C. Hole]] is corrupt and that he liked to take bribes from the mob.{{Ref|name=NedBurner}} He goes on to offer him $200,000 to expose Hole's corrupt ways to the public and end his lies to satisfy him.

A few months later, Carrington flies to [[Francis International Airport (3D Universe)|Francis International Airport]] to meet with the [[Colombian Cartel]] and discuss plans to re-develop the crime-ravaged, gang warfare-ridden, Mafia-controlled district of [[Fort Staunton]] on [[Staunton Island]]. Carrington's former protégé, Donald Love, hears of his impending arrival and hires [[Toni Cipriani]] to kill Carrington and acquire his real estate plans, as the [[Panlantic Construction Company|Panlantic Corporation]] would 'do anything to get prime real estate'. Cipriani later [[Morgue Party Resurrection|steals]] Carrington's corpse for Love, who eats the body on his flight away from Liberty City.

==Trivia==
===General===
*His surname is possibly inspired by the {{WP|Carrington family}} from the popular 1980s TV show ''{{WP|Dynasty (1981 TV series)|Dynasty}}''.
*Avery bears visual similarities to {{WC|kingofthehill|M.F. Thatherton}}, a propane businessman from the {{WP|Fox Broadcasting Company|Fox}} cartoon series ''{{WP|King Of The Hill}}''. Both are voiced by the late [[Burt Reynolds]].

===''Grand Theft Auto''===
*Avery Carrington is one of the three real estate developers in the {{S}} to be named Avery. The others are [[Lenny Avery]] and [[Avery Duggan]].
*Avery's missions are not necessary for the story, as they can be completed even after the final mission.
*All of his missions in {{VC}}, apart from [[Demolition Man]], require the player to have a change of [[Clothing in GTA Vice City|clothes]].
*It is revealed in [[Morgue Party Resurrection]] that he was wearing a wig, which confirms both [[Tommy Vercetti]]'s and the [[Vice City Bureau of Investigation|VCBI]]'s suspicions.
*Avery's character model appears to be unchanged from his ''GTA Vice City'' appearance during the {{LCS}} [[Missions in GTA Liberty City Stories|mission]] [[Panlantic Land Grab]], despite the [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories/Artwork|artwork]] depicting him having significantly aged.
**On the mobile version, his character model was updated and he now looks similar to his artwork.
*Originally, Avery's first name was Sergio. His missions still have the prefix "''SERG''" in the game files of ''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City''.
