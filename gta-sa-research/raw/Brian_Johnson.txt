'''Brian Johnson''' is a character who is mentioned in {{SA}}.

==History==
===Background===
====Early life====
Brian was the youngest known son of [[Beverly Johnson]] and the younger brother of [[Carl Johnson|Carl]], [[Sweet Johnson|Sean]] and [[Kendl Johnson]]. It is implied that he bore a strong resemblance to Carl, as [[Emmet]] initially mistook Carl for Brian when they first met. According to Carl's flashbacks, it appears the two were close, and enjoyed creating mischief together.

Brian was associated with the [[Grove Street Families]] due to being the younger brother of the gang's leader, "Sweet" Sean, but his role within the gang was unknown. Brian lived in the [[Johnson House]] in [[Ganton]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], until his death.

====Death====
Brian died in [[1987]]. The cause of his death was never revealed, though it is implied that he was killed by a rival gang and Carl was present but he did not attempt to help Brian.

Sweet blamed Carl for Brian's death, which resulted in Carl moving to [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]]. Carl did not attend Brian's funeral, which Sweet confirms by saying: "''That's another funeral you ran away from, fool. Just like Brian's.''"

Brian died at the age of 10, according to pre-release Rockstar information provided to PlayStation Magazine articles.{{Ref|name=rnorthinfo}}

===Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''===
Brian's death plays a major role in Carl and Sweet's relationship.

In [[1992]], Carl returns to Los Santos after his mother, Beverly, was killed by the Ballas. He [[Sweet & Kendl|arrived]] at the funeral after it had ended, making Sweet angry at Carl for missing another one.

[[Frank Tenpenny|Officer Tenpenny]] later mentions Brian when he, [[Eddie Pulaski|Pulaski]] and [[Jimmy Hernandez|Hernandez]] kidnap Carl and [[The Green Sabre|take]] him out to [[Angel Pine]]. CJ expresses his disappointment over [[Big Smoke|Big Smoke's]] betrayal, to which Tenpenny makes his point by saying Carl and Smoke were never homies for life since Smoke 'learned his lesson a long time ago' and began working with [[C.R.A.S.H.]] and not Grove Street because it made more sense and also because he and the rest of the gang ran him out of town for letting Brian die, showing that Tenpenny is aware of Brian's death and agrees that it was CJ's fault. Although, most of this appears to just be Tenpenny being cruel and disrespectful, since it's never explicitly stated anywhere else that the gang actually ran CJ out of Los Santos. In addition, while Big Smoke himself was somewhat disappointed that Carl left, he did not seem to hold him accountable for Brian's death (or at least doesn't mention it) unlike Sweet who blamed Carl but did not want him to leave. Sweet also doesn't mention running Carl off in the Introduction but is nonetheless angry with him going to Liberty City. It's also worth noting that, ruse or not, Smoke was the only major member of the families who was happy that CJ returned, unlike [[Ryder]] who openly doubts and insults him, presumably because he also considered CJ to be weak for leaving. By all accounts, CJ left of his own accord because he could not take the pressure and to avoid responsibility for what happened to Brian. All of which proves that CJ abandoned Grove Street and not the other way around.

When Carl and Sweet are waiting outside [[Big Smoke's Crack Palace]] just before they [[End of the Line|attack it]], Sweet attempts to follow Carl, but Carl tells him that he has to go in alone and that he is doing this for Brian, as he "let him die".
