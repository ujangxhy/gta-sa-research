'''Safehouses''' (also known as '''hideouts''' in {{III}} and {{A}}) are buildings which house the [[Save Points|save point]] that the [[protagonist]] must physically enter in order for the player to save the game. Safehouses are a subset of save points, as they are more than just a floating save icon. Some safehouses offer [[garages]] to store vehicles and a place to change the player's [[clothing]].

Prior to {{SA}}, most save points were outside safehouses. In {{CW}}, some safehouses include seclusion where the player can take hijacked vehicles with stolen contents to search.
