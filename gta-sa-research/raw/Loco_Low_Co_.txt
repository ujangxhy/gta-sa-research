'''Loco Low Co.''' is an automobile customization shop appearing in {{SA}}.

==Description==
The mod shop is located in the neighborhood of [[Willowfield]] in [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], and specializes in customizing vehicles that are categorized as "lowriders"; these vehicles have built-in hydraulic pumps and cylinders within their chassis that allow the vehicle to adjust height and bounce.

Lowriders get visible additions as well: cylinders and suspension mods from the vehicle's axles to the chassis (best seen at when all hydraulics are deployed). In addition to hydraulics, the garage offers a myriad of other customizations that vary between each lowrider. Once hydraulics are equipped, the horn ability is removed and replaced with the hydraulic bounce.

Similarly to all other available mod shops, the effects of changing colors at Loco Low Co. is similar to [[Pay 'n' Spray]], repairing the vehicle and clearing any [[Wanted Level in GTA San Andreas|wanted level]]. However, the cost of each individual color change is consistently $150, 1.5× higher than a Pay 'n' Spray service, regardless of whether or not the car is damaged to begin with or the player has a wanted level.

==Availability==
The Loco Low Co. mod shop becomes available to the player after the completion of the mission [[Cesar Vialpando (mission)|Cesar Vialpando]], which serves as an introductory mission for mod shops.
