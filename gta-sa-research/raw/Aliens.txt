'''Aliens''', also known as '''extraterrestrials''', are creatures that did not originate on Earth. They appear in {{V}} as [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V|Easter eggs]] and figments of [[Michael De Santa|Michael's]] imagination, and in {{O}} during a [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA Online#Gunrunning|secret]] resupply mission.

==Description==
Their bodies are similar to the {{WC|half-life|Vortigaunt|Vortigaunts}} from the ''{{WC|half-life|Half-Life series|Half Life}}'' series, while their elongated head is reminiscent of the {{WC|avp|xenomorph|Xenomorphs}} from the ''{{WP|Alien (franchise)|Alien}}'' franchise. They have green skin, large crescent-shaped heads, and large mouths with big teeth.

==Aliens in Older ''GTA''s==
*{{1}} - Mentioned briefly during [[Heist Almighty - Penetrator]]: the [[Protagonists in GTA (1997 game)|protagonist]] is tasked to rescue the [[Vercotti Crime Family contact|Vercotti Family contact's]] associate [[Mason]] who invaded an Army base, and when they pick him up, he claim he's got secret files regarding aliens.
*{{SA}} - Reptilian aliens are spoken of by some [[pedestrians]] and the [[Area 69 (3D Universe)|Area 69]] base intercom mentions an alien body during the [[Black Project|mission]] there. According to the [[Epsilon Program (3D Universe)|Epsilon Program]], aliens are present on Earth. There is also a small bar near Area 69 called the [[Lil' Probe'Inn]] which has an alien/UFO theme.
*{{IV}} and {{EFLC}} - Some pedestrians (mostly the [[Tramps|homeless ones]]) ramble about aliens.

==Appearances in ''Grand Theft Auto V''==
===[[Prologue]]===
[[File:Frozen Alien-Prologue-GTAV.png|thumb|170px]]
The mission guides the player to drive ahead over the [[Freight Train|railroads]] but instead, turn right and go under the bridge, stop the car, get out, and the player will notice that there's a frozen alien in the river.

===[[Franklin and Lamar]]===
[[File:Aliens-Franklin and Lamar-GTAV.png|thumb|170px]]
Aliens also appear during the mission [[Franklin and Lamar]], where an alien movie, [[Republican Space Rangers]] is being filmed. They are actually not aliens but people costumed into aliens while the movie casting takes place, and they could be more or less an [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V|Easter Egg]]. At the same time, [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] and [[Lamar Davis|Lamar]] are racing through the [[Richards Majestic Productions|movie studio]] at high speeds. Not hitting any of the alien-dressed actors will count for the 100% mission score, checking the "''We come in peace''" box. The same actors wearing alien costumes can be found when exploring the movie studio in free roam.

===[[Grass Roots]]===
[[File:Aliens-Grass Roots-GTAV.png|thumb|170px]]
Playing the mission as [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] will trigger a hallucination after smoking [[Weed|pot]]. Michael's vision and hearing become blurred and the world around him appears to be in a haze. He is suddenly hefting a minigun as aliens begin to appear all around him. Michael must defeat the aliens without dying himself.

[[Barry (GTA V)|Barry]], the man who gives Michael weed in the mission, refers to the aliens as "monsters" (the fact that Trevor hallucinates about clowns instead indicates the "monsters" might vary depending on the person).

===[[Did Somebody Say Yoga?]]===
[[File:Abduction-Did Somebody Say Yoga?-GTAV.png|thumb|170px]]
Michael goes with [[Jimmy De Santa|Jimmy]] to a [[Burger Shot (HD Universe)|Burger Shot]] to score weed and a "special drink" from his dealer who works there. On their way back home, Michael experiences the onset of a psychedelic trip after taking a sip of Jimmy's ketamine-spiked drink. Jimmy then forces his father out of the [[Tailgater]], and drives off. Michael then hallucinates being surrounded by [[chimpanzees]], abducted by aliens, and then dropped off midair floating over the [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|city]].

===[[Bury the Hatchet]]===
The alien frozen under the railway bridge seen in the [[Prologue]] is still there in this mission. To see it, just follow the same steps as above.

===Other Appearances===
*In [[Sandy Shores]], near the [[Freight Train|train]] tunnel and across from the [[Sandy Shores Airfield|airstrip]], there is an [[Beam Me Up|area 'dedicated' to aliens]]. It is a mound which has been decorated/painted with alien faces and shapes with numerous phrases such as 'Save Us' and 'Beam Me Up'. There is also a rusted, unusable [[Regina]] with a model UFO on its roof. There are generally a few [[Camper (HD Universe)|Campers]] or [[Surfer]]s parked nearby too, suggesting this is more of a 'hippy camp' type area, rather than a community thing.
*West of the [[Redwood Lights Track]] in the [[Grand Senora Desert]] is a house marked by an [[United States of America|American]] flag in the front yard. In the back yard is a small gathering of 'hippies' and a painted banner which reads 'WELCOME' next to a green alien head.
*In the enhanced version of ''GTA V'', in the [[Director Mode]] of the [[Rockstar Editor]], the player can control and use one of the actors that was using the alien costume during the mission [[Franklin and Lamar]].
*A movie called "[[Los Santos 2020]]" features an alien.
*There is a statue of an alien's head in [[Solomon Richards|Solomon Richards']] office in bookcase. It can be seen during the [[Murder Mystery]] side-mission when the player has access to free-roam his office.
*The player can see an [[AlienProber3]] movie poster and magazine inside [[Lester's House]].

==Appearances in ''Grand Theft Auto Online''==
[[File:AlienResupply-GTAOnline.png|The crashed UFO during the resupply job.|350px|right]]
As part of the [[GTA Online: Gunrunning|Gunrunning]] update, a special resupply job was added where during the night a crashed flying saucer surrounded by several dead [[Military (HD Universe)|Military]] personnel and scientists, as well as abandoned [[Barracks OL|trucks]], [[Rhino (HD Universe)|tanks]] and [[FIB (vehicle)|SUVs]] can be found. When the player takes the "supply" (an alien egg), several aliens will suddenly appear before the player. They are not hostile and disappear when aimed at.

This resupply job may appear once the player has completed at least 600 resupplies and started a new supply run between 21:00 and 23:00. Due to this event being randomly scripted, it is not guaranteed to appear as soon as the player has completed 600 resupplies.

As a reference to the theory that aliens anal probe the people that they capture, the bodies of the scientists and military personnel scattered around the crash site have blood seeping through their clothes around their anal areas.

==Transportation==
{{Main|UFOs}}
As a method of transportation the aliens use UFOs, or referred to popularly as "flying saucers" or "flying discs". One appears during Michael's hallucination in the mission [[Did Somebody Say Yoga?]], which is used to do experiments on human beings. A sunken UFO can be found at the north of the map at the bottom of the sea. Also, after achieving [[100% Completion in GTA V|100% Completion]], a UFO can be seen on top of [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]] during stormy [[weather]]. Oddly, it has "[[Federal Investigation Bureau|FIB]]" written on it, which implies it is actually government property. This UFO model is identical to the sunken one, albeit less dirty. The very same UFO model also appears above the [[Beam Me Up]] location in Sandy Shores. A very different style of UFO appears high above [[Fort Zancudo]], with its location marked with a spinning green light that appears on top of one of the fort's buildings from 3AM to 4AM. This UFO is also heavily implied to be government property, as its outside cabin features hazard warnings written in English. It also features transparent glass, through which a cockpit area containing two humanoid beings operating the UFO can be seen (this can be seen clearly when viewing the UFO's 3D model)<ref>{{URL|https://youtu.be/vYfOBaD1b1k&t{{=}}373|GTA 5 - Jetpack and the Mystery of Mount Chiliad - Hot Topic #3}}</ref>.

==Mount Chiliad Drawing==
{{Main|Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)#Points of Interest}}
A mysterious drawing on a wall in the Mount Chiliad [[Cable Car|cable car]] station appears to show the mountain with lightning emanating from it and an odd symbol, possibly an eye or a UFO, on top. Other notable details on the drawing are depictions of a UFO, a person on a jetpack, a cracked egg, and multiple boxes with Xs in the middle of them. Nothing else is known about the meaning of the drawing.

Under the observation platform on top of the mountain is the same symbol featured on top of the drawing. Carved on the side of the platform are the words "Come back when your story is complete". This serves only to inform the player that they must reach 100% completion in order to see the UFO. This same symbol is also painted on the side of the mountain and in the Beam Me Up area in Sandy Shores, along with a drawing of a rain cloud and the moon, another way to inform the player of the UFOs viewing requirements, in this case being stormy weather and the time being 3AM.
