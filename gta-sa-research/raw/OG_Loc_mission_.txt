'''OG Loc''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[Grove Street Families]] member [[Big Smoke|Melvin "Big Smoke" Harris]] from his [[Big Smoke's House|home]] in [[Idlewood]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Mission==
Carl goes to visit Big Smoke at his house in Idlewood, but there is no answer at the door. Just then, [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet]] and Big Smoke are seen walking down the driveway and greet Carl. Sweet and Smoke tell Carl that they have to go to the [[Los Santos Police Headquarters|police station]] in [[Pershing Square]] to pick up their friend [[OG Loc (3D Universe)|Jeffrey]], who was imprisoned for joyriding and parking fines. On the way to the police station, Smoke tells Carl that Jeffrey is now called "OG Loc".

When they arrive at the police station, Loc is waiting outside at the front of the building. Carl calls Loc by his real name Jeffrey, which annoys him. Sweet and Smoke then mock him for not going to college. Loc then tells them they have to go see a "Cholo motherfucker" named [[Freddy (GTA San Andreas)|Freddy]] in [[East Los Santos (3D Universe)|East Los Santos]], who apparently stole Loc's rhymes book. Freddy will say "you dropped the soap, sugar" and "it was just a prison thing", indirectly revealing he was sexually abused while in prison, much to Carl's amusement.

In the car, en route to East Los Santos, Loc tells the others he got a job at [[Burger Shot (3D Universe)|Burger Shot]], courtesy of his parole officer. They soon arrive at the [[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]] member's house. Sweet and Smoke leave while Carl stays with Loc. Carl and Loc confront Freddy at [[Freddy's House|his house]], who simply says that it was "a prison thing" and then escapes outside via the back door onto a [[PCJ-600|motorbike]]. Carl and Loc quickly jump onto another motorbike and give chase to Freddy throughout the streets, alleyways and freeways of [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]]. Freddy soon takes off down the railway tracks and stops at a [[Basketball|basketball court]] where more Vagos members are awaiting Carl and Loc's arrival. Carl and Loc get into a small shootout with the Vagos members, who are killed along with Freddy. Loc then asks Carl to give him a lift to the [[Burger Shot (3D Universe)|Burger Shot]] in [[Marina (3D Universe)|Marina]]. Carl agrees and drives Loc to the fast-food joint. Loc tells Carl that he will see him around and heads inside while Carl leaves.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Pick up OG Loc from the <font color="#D1BC86">'''police station'''</font>.
*Drive to <font color="#D1BC86">'''Freddy's house'''</font>.
*Go and ring the <font color="#D1BC86">'''doorbell'''</font>.
*Catch and kill <font color="#AB3736">'''Freddy'''</font>!
*Get on the <font color="#484576">'''bike'''</font>.<small>{{H:title|If the player leaves the bike.|[?]}}</small>
*You have left <font color="#484576">'''OG Loc'''</font> behind. <small>{{H:title|If the player gets too far away from OG Loc.|[?]}}</small>
*Take OG Loc to the employees' door at the back of the <font color="#D1BC86">'''Burger Shot'''</font>.

==Deaths==
*[[Freddy (GTA San Andreas)|Freddy]] - Killed by either OG Loc or Carl Johnson for embarrassing Loc in prison.
*[[Los Santos Vagos (3D Universe)|Vagos]] members (optional) - Can be killed if Freddy joins them after the chase.

==Reward==
The reward for this mission is an increase in [[respect]]. The missions [[Running Dog]] and [[Life's a Beach]] are unlocked.

==Notes==
*Freddy's [[PCJ 600|PCJ-600]] is a [[Special Vehicles in GTA San Andreas|special variant]] that is impervious to bullets, fire, explosions, melee attacks, and collisions. The player can acquire it towards the end of the mission: Carl must get off the motorbike used to pursue Freddy and then go to where Freddy is waiting. Once Freddy starts firing at CJ, the player needs to kill OG Loc all the while Freddy is kept in sight. Killing OG Loc fails the mission, after which Freddy will try to escape with the PCJ-600, which is when the player can knock him off and obtain the bike.

==Trivia==
*This is the first mission that uses the [[Trip Skip]] feature if the player has failed the mission beforehand.
*In the [[cutscene]] when picking up Jeffrey, Big Smoke's car will sometimes be white, instead of the usual blue color. When the cutscene ends, the Glendale is back to being blue again. This is due to the typo error in the <code>carcols.dat</code> file. It can be fixed on the [[PC]] version of the game by replacing the period with a comma<ref>{{URL|https://youtu.be/J2Mljm9E8-w?t{{=}}400|5 Things You Didn't Know in GTA San Andreas}}</ref>. This is fixed in the remastered versions.
*After Carl and OG Loc are dropped off at Freddy's house, as Big Smoke drives away, he may sometimes accidentally run over OG Loc, depending on how the player initially parked the car.
*When aiming at OG Loc as he is at Freddy's door, he will start attacking Carl.
*After getting on the bike and entering the first alley, a dead person Freddy killed will always be seen next to an onlooker.
*After the cutscene, Big Smoke and Sweet will head back to the hood. They can be killed with guns, the Rocket Launcher or the "[[Cheats in GTA San Andreas|Blow up all cars]]" cheat, but this will fail the mission.
*During the chase, Freddy is immune to gunshots until he moves.
*If the player manages to pull Freddy off his bike while on foot without killing him, the game will crash.
*During this mission, Freddy drives his PCJ-600 alone, while still able to shoot in any direction. This feature is neither available to CJ in the game (CJ can only shoot ahead or to the side, while driving the bike), nor ever done by any other character.
*It is possible to lose OG Loc by crashing the PCJ-600. Carl will then be on his own to kill Freddy. However, even if this happens and Carl kills Freddy before he reaches the basketball court, the cutscene where OG Loc points a gun at him after his death will appear.
*Freddy can also be shot at by Vagos or Ballas gang members while cruising on the motorcycle attracting attention by shooting. He can also be killed by other gang members (especially Ballas) when he dismounts his bike and becomes accompanied by the Vagos members.
*Failing this mission at any time without being [[wasted]] or [[busted]] in the progress will allow Carl to enter nearby houses that normally are accessible only in Burglary missions, but once he exits the house he will not be allowed to enter again. If doing this at Freddy's house, Carl will have a chance to enter it, or nearby houses. (Confirmed in the early PC version, patched in later PC, mobile and re-mastered versions)
*At the beginning of the chase, Freddy will stay still until the player gets close enough. During that time, Freddy cannot be killed and the PCJ-600 he drives is completely indestructible. These effects lasts for several seconds after he starts driving (where he does his 2nd turn after he drives through the alley).
**However, in the remastered versions, both Freddy and his bike are invincible all the time so the player cannot do a quick finish during the chase and can only be killed once he leaves his PCJ-600 at the basketball court.
*At the end, when OG Loc says "like a quarter pounder" he may be referencing {{WP|McDonald's}} {{wP|Quarter Pounder}}.
*CJ has an unused line for this mission for when he and OG Loc pursue Freddy, expressing his worry about OG Loc potentially getting caught violating his parole<ref>{{URL|https://tcrf.net/Grand_Theft_Auto:_San_Andreas/Unused_Mission_Related_Text_and_Audio/Los_Santos#OG_Loc|The Cutting Room Floor}}</ref>.
