'''Just Business''' is a mission in {{SA}} given to [[protagonist]] [[Carl Johnson]] by [[Grove Street Families]] member [[Big Smoke|Melvin "Big Smoke" Harris]] from his [[Big Smoke's House|home]] in [[Idlewood]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]].

==Mission==
Carl drops by Big Smoke's house and finds him in the garage. They agree to take a ride somewhere, as Smoke has business to take care of. They head to [[Commerce]] and Big Smoke enters the [[Atrium]]. After a brief moment, a shootout is heard inside, and Carl quickly rushes inside, protecting Smoke from attacking [[Russian Mafia (3D Universe)|Russian Mafia]] goons. Once all of the Russians are killed, the two hop on a [[BF-400]] and attempt to escape. Carl will use the [[Micro SMG (3D Universe)|Micro SMG]] with unlimited ammo and acts as the shooter, killing as many enemies as possible. On the way, they jump over the ramp of a [[Packer]] to escape a roadblock, causing all of the Russians there to be obliterated in a huge explosion, however the bike survives. After a long chase, Big Smoke rides through an alley in [[East Beach]]. The two have lost all the Russians, agreeing to split up.

==Mission Objectives==
In order to complete the mission the player must:
*Take Big Smoke to <font color=#D1BC86>'''Downtown'''</font>.
*Protect <font color=#484576>'''Big Smoke'''</font> from the <font color=#AB3736>'''Russians'''</font>.
*Follow <font color=#484576>'''Big Smoke'''</font> and protect him from the <font color=#AB3736>'''Russians'''</font>.
*Protect the bike from the <font color=#AB3736>'''Russians'''</font>.

==Trivia==
*The song at the start of the opening [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] is "The Vapors" by {{WP|Biz Markie}}. This song can also be heard on [[Playback FM]].
*The woman [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] flirts with when he arrives at the [[Atrium]] bears a strong resemblance to {{WP|Catherine Tramell}} from the movie ''{{WP|Basic Instinct}}''.
*Sound files suggest that CJ was supposed to enter the [[Atrium]] by player control instead of a cutscene, as the mission's sound script has a few lines for Big Smoke, such as ''"CJ, get in here!"'' and ''"What took you so long?"''.{{Ref|name=playercontrol|type=website|ref={{URL|https://tcrf.net/Grand_Theft_Auto:_San_Andreas/Unused_Mission_Related_Text_and_Audio/Los_Santos|The Cutting Room Floor}}}}
*When CJ and [[Big Smoke]] get onto the [[BF-400]] to escape, the radio starts playing [[Radio X]].
*The player's [[SMG (3D Universe)|SMG]] weapon slot is replaced with a [[Micro SMG (3D Universe)|Micro SMG]] when the gunfight begins, and once again when the motorbike chase begins with one providing infinite ammo.
**Therefore, there is no point to save SMG ammo, as it will be reset to 80 after this mission.
**During the bike chase, if the player activates a [[Cheats in GTA San Andreas|cheat]] to change the Micro SMG into either a [[Tec-9]] or an SMG, they will not only be able to shoot with a different weapon, but will receive infinite ammo for the Micro SMG after the mission is over.
*Enabling the "health and armor" cheat during the bike chase will blow up Big Smoke's bike, failing the mission, and CJ will eventually be [[wasted]]. 
*The scene in the mission featuring a [[Packer]] jumping off a surface road in slow motion into a storm drain channel, which Big Smoke and CJ are using to escape, is a nod to a similar scene in the 1991 film ''{{WP|Terminator 2: Judgment Day}}'', where a large tow-truck (driven by a Terminator) executes a similar stunt as it is in pursuit of John Connor (also riding a motorcycle). This scene was also featured in the second ''GTA San Andreas'' [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas/Trailers|trailer]].
*The BF-400 in this mission has the [[Vehicle Features/Custom Vehicle License Plates|license plate]] reading "IMY AK" (similarly to the [[Cheetah (3D Universe)|Cheetah]] the player can buy at the [[Easter Basin]] docks and the [[Feltzer]] in [[End of the Line]]).
*The stunt jumps executed by Big Smoke in this mission will be included in the player's [[Statistics in GTA San Andreas|stats]], even though Carl is not driving.
*This mission is similar to {{TLAD}} mission [[Shifting Weight]] where [[Johnny Klebitz|Johnny]] shoots at the [[Liberty City Police Department (HD Universe)|LCPD]] while on a [[Double T Custom|motorcycle]] and acting as the shooter (since Johnny's [[Hexer|bike]] got destroyed before the chase).
