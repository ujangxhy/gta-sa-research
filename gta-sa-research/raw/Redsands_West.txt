}}'''Redsands West''' is a district in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]]. It is bordered by the districts of [[Whitewood Estates]] to the west, [[Prickle Pine]] to the north and [[Redsands East]] to the east.

==Description==
Redsands West provides great access to other places, because the district is nearby numerous means of transportation; Las Venturas' two main freeways, the [[Harry Gold Parkway]] and the [[Julius Thruway]] service the area. The [[Pilson Intersection]] is northwest of the district, although it does not really service Redsands West. Also, to the immediate south of the district, is the [[Las Venturas Airport]], providing flights from Las Venturas to either [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]] and [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]], and vice versa.

Redsands West can be described as a residential, industrial and commercial district. The area is diverse and has a large hispanic population. It houses an abattoir, some businesses, fire station, single family homes, apartments, and a casino and a baseball stadium for recreation.

==Influence==
The district appears to be inspired by {{WP|North Las Vegas, Nevada|North Las Vegas}} and {{WP|West Las Vegas}} in {{WP|Nevada}}.
