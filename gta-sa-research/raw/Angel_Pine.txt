'''Angel Pine''' is a small rural town located in [[Whetstone]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]] in {{SA}}.

==Description==
Located in [[Whetstone]], Angel Pine is a small, rural town nestled between [[Mount Chiliad (3D Universe)|Mount Chiliad]] and the forests of [[Shady Creek]]. It boasts an amazing view of the mountain and is virtually a minute away from the eeriness of [[Shady Cabin]] (situated in the heart of [[Shady Creeks]]) and minutes away from [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] via the highway to northern [[Foster Valley]].

The primary industry in the town is the [[Angel Pine Sawmill]]. There is a [[Save Point|save point]] in the Angel Pine trailer park and the [[Angel Pine Safehouse]], which can be bought for [[Money|$]] 20,000. The town features a [[Angel Pine Sheriff's Station|Sheriff`s Office]] and a [[Angel Pine Medical Center|medical center]]. The population is mainly working class whites and farmers.

==Influence==
Angel Pine appears to be based on [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulder_Creek,_California Boulder Creek, California], considering the layout of the town and the town being within a densely forested area with mountainous hills rising above the community, and the relatively close proximity to San Francisco, even including a liquor store arrow sign in Angel Pine that appears similar to [https://www.roadarch.com/20/7/johnnies.jpg a sign] in Boulder Creek, although also sharing similarities, as well as a similar name, with [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lone_Pine%2C_California Lone Pine, California], and, considering a single significantly large mountain rising above the community, also being similar to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weed,_California Weed, California].

==Events of ''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas''==
{{Spoiler}}
After the deadly [[Mulholland Intersection]] [[The Green Sabre|shoot-out]] with the [[Ballas (3D Universe)|Ballas]] in [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], [[Carl Johnson]] is forced by [[C.R.A.S.H.]] officers [[Frank Tenpenny]] and [[Eddie Pulaski]] to perform various tasks for them, using the recent incarceration of Carl's brother [[Sweet Johnson|Sweet]] as leverage. Tenpenny and Pulaski deposit Carl in the remote town of Angel Pine in [[Whetstone]], a region of [[Flint County (3D Universe)|Flint County]] in [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]]. Carl, who has been stripped of all weapons and given only a [[Camera|camera]], is ordered to [[Badlands|flush out and murder]] a [[The Informant|witness]] who is hiding in a cabin on nearby [[Mount Chiliad (3D Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. He rushes up the side of the mountain to find a cabin closely guarded by the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation|FBI]]. By creating a commotion, Carl succeeds in luring the terrified witness from the cabin, who races down the mountain in a [[Washington|car]]. After chasing him down, Carl kills him and photographs his body as proof for Tenpenny and Pulaski that their problem has been taken care of.

After [[Catalina]]'s first robbery, [[Carl Johnson|CJ]] receives a call from a strange subject, it turns out to be The Truth. During the call, [[The Truth]] asks him to go to a Hotel in Angel Pine. Carl arrives at the Hotel, and there is Tenpenny smoking a bong. Then, The Truth appears, and Tenpenny leaves the Hotel and tells him to talk a while with Carl. The Truth tells Carl that he has problems with some farmers and to steal their combine harvester.

After Carl takes the [[Combine Harvester]] to The Truth's Farm, he receives a call from Cesar. The latter tells Carl that his [[Varrios Los Aztecas (3D Universe)|gang]] is dissolved, so he had to leave Los Santos, and now he and [[Kendl Johnson|Kendl]] are in [[Angel Pine Save Point|CJ's trailer]]. Once CJ reaches the trailer, Cesar tells him that he intends to return back in Los Santos to restore the order in the neighborhood, but Carl and Kendl dissuade him from doing so. Carl tries to calm Cesar, but him reminds and explains to Carl that Big Smoke and the C.R.A.S.H. team are behind the drug dealing. Carl responds to Cesar with incredulity, arguing that Big Smoke would never get involved in drug dealing, and that the drug epidemic is the fault of the C.R.A.S.H. team. Both Kendl and Cesar try to convince Carl of Smoke's responsibility for the dealing and Balla/LSPD ambush of the Grove Street Families, but Carl is still unwilling to believe them. Deciding to monitor the highway from San Fierro to Los Santos for any suspicious activity, Carl leaves the trailer after telling the pair to lay low for a while.

Carl meets Cesar and then they arrive at Angel Pine, where the Ballas were last seen heading. B and Mike Toreno]] as they head inside the [[Cluckin' Bell (3D Universe)|Cluckin' Bell]] outlet. Carl then drives to a nearby petrol bunk and then Carl and Cesar split up.

During the mission [[Lure (GTA San Andreas)|Lure]], Carl is working for the [[San Fierro Triads]] when one of the gang's leaders, [[Ran Fa Li]], is targeted by the [[Da Nang Boys]], and Carl offers himself as a decoy to lure them out of the city. Carl drives the decoy car to the [[Angel Pine Sawmill]], where a group of ambushers on bikes begin to chase and attack the car from behind. Carl eventually stops after [[Woozie]] has successfully taken Ran Fa Li into safety, after which the bikers realize it was a decoy.
<gallery widths="210" position="center">

Later in the story, Angel Pine returns, this time as the setting for the [[N.O.E.|air-drop]] Carl is asked to perform by [[Mike Toreno]]. In order to stay within the diligent gaze of those watching him, Toreno compels Carl to fly a [[Rustler]] to Angel Pine for him in order to drop supplies to his men holed up there. Carl is given a very limited time to fly over topographically diverse terrain from the [[Verdant Meadows Airfield|abandoned airstrip]] in [[Bone County (3D Universe)|Bone County]] to Angel Pine and back, all while remaining below radar detection range.
