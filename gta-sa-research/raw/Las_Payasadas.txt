'''Las Payasadas''' is a town located in northern [[Bone County (3D Universe)|Bone County]], [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]], a few miles away from [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]], that appears in {{SA}}.

==Description==
Road signage indicates that the town has a population of 211 in [[1992]]. The town sits on a cliff, with [[Verdant Meadows Airfield|Verdant Meadows]] visible in the valley below. The town's most prominent feature is a large model chicken known as [[The Big Pecker]], the mascot of [[Pecker's Feed & Seed]] company, with a constantly bobbing head. When entering the town from the either side (east or west), a sign is visible, reading: ''"Las Payasadas, home of "The Big Pecker", The World's Largest Cock"''.

The name ''Las Payasadas'' translates roughly from Spanish to ''horseplay'', ''clowning around'', or ''antics'', a possible reference to the lack of seriousness afforded to this town owing to its giant chicken.

==Influence==
The town appears to resemble {{WP|Goodsprings, Nevada}}, with the layout and appearance of the town and general area, the town's proximity to Las Venturas (which is based on {{WP|Las Vegas}}), and the town's close proximity to the north of a remote airstrip (Verdant Meadows, which may be based on {{WP|Sky Ranch Airport (Nevada)|Sky Ranch Airport}}).

==Trivia==
*Las Payasadas along with [[Montgomery (3D Universe)|Montgomery]], [[Las Barrancas]] and [[Bayside]] are the only rural/small towns in [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|San Andreas]] that don't have a purchasable [[Save Points in GTA San Andreas|safehouse]].
*In the map that comes with the game, the town is misspelled as ''"Las Payasdas"''. Another error is shown on a sign in [[Valle Ocultado]], reading the town's name as "La Payasadas".
*Before the western approach into this town, there is a small dirt road that leads to the road leading to an unused building. This road, although small, has an unusually high volume of traffic using it.
